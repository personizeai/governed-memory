# TechFlow Inc — Prospect Research

## Company Overview
TechFlow Inc is a B2B SaaS company specializing in workflow automation and data orchestration solutions for enterprise teams. Founded in 2018 and headquartered in San Francisco, the company helps organizations streamline complex processes across sales, marketing, and operations. TechFlow's platform emphasizes scalability and integration with modern cloud ecosystems, positioning it as a key player in the growing no-code/low-code automation market.

As of recent data, TechFlow employs approximately 280-310 people, with LinkedIn listing 312 employees—though this figure may be slightly stale based on direct insights from leadership (source: LinkedIn; Sarah Chen, VP of Engineering). The company maintains a lean, engineering-focused culture amid rapid growth, operating primarily in the US with a distributed team across engineering, product, and sales functions. TechFlow targets mid-market to enterprise clients in tech, finance, and healthcare sectors, boasting strong retention rates and expanding into international markets (source: company website; LinkedIn).

## Funding & Financials
TechFlow has secured a total of $68M in funding across three rounds, reflecting strong investor confidence in its growth trajectory (source: CrunchBase). The most recent raise was a $45M Series C in September 2024, led by Sequoia Capital, with participation from existing investors including a16z and Bessemer Venture Partners. This round valued the company at over $300M post-money and is earmarked for accelerating product development, international expansion, and sales hires.

Prior rounds include a $15M Series B in 2022 and $8M Series A in 2020, both focused on core platform enhancements. Financially, TechFlow reports ARR exceeding $50M as of Q3 2024, with projections for 2.5x YoY growth into 2025. The company remains bootstrapped on operational profitability in key segments, minimizing burn rate while scaling aggressively (source: CrunchBase; PitchBook; TechFlow investor deck summaries). No public debt or secondary sales noted.

## Technology & Engineering
TechFlow's engineering stack is modern and polyglot, with heavy reliance on TypeScript for frontend and full-stack applications, alongside Java for backend services and data processing pipelines. Their GitHub organization reveals over 50 public repositories, showcasing active contributions in these languages, including SDKs for integrations and open-source tooling (source: GitHub — TechFlow org).

The company is midway through a significant microservices migration initiative, as detailed in their tech blog, transitioning from a monolith to a Kubernetes-orchestrated architecture on AWS for improved resilience and deployment velocity (source: TechFlow tech blog). Glassdoor reviews highlight impressive engineering velocity—often citing "ship fast, iterate faster" as a cultural mantra—but note gaps in internal tooling, such as CI/CD pipelines and observability stacks, which some teams are actively addressing (source: Glassdoor).

Under VP of Engineering Sarah Chen, the team emphasizes DevOps best practices and AI-driven automation, with recent hires in platform engineering to support this evolution. TechFlow's platform integrates natively with major data warehouses and ETL tools, underscoring its data-centric approach.
