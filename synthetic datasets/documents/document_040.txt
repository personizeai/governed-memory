# ClassGrid — Account Overview

## Company Background
ClassGrid is a leading EdTech company specializing in innovative digital platforms for classroom management, student engagement, and administrative tools tailored for K-12 and higher education institutions. With approximately 452 employees—falling squarely within the 201-1000 company size range—they serve over 5,000 schools across North America and Europe, enabling teachers to streamline lesson planning, track student progress in real-time, and foster collaborative learning environments through AI-driven analytics and interactive modules. Founded in 2015 by a trio of former public school educators in Seattle who were frustrated with fragmented legacy systems that failed to keep pace with modern digital-native students, ClassGrid bootstrapped its way to Series B funding by 2019, pivoting from a simple grading app to a comprehensive ecosystem amid the rise of remote learning during the pandemic. Today, the company grapples with scaling challenges, including high infrastructure costs on AWS that are eating into margins, security concerns with their current architecture, and a recent board meeting last month that shifted priorities toward cost optimization and faster go-to-market for new features. Their tech stack is robust and modern, leveraging Go for backend services, Kubernetes for orchestration, AWS for cloud hosting, DynamoDB for NoSQL storage, and gRPC for efficient microservices communication.

## Technical Requirements
- **Seamless Integration with Existing Stack**: Must support native compatibility with Go backend services, Kubernetes orchestration (EKS on AWS), DynamoDB for scalable NoSQL data handling, and gRPC protocols for low-latency microservices communication; no vendor lock-in or heavy refactoring.
- **Security Enhancements**: Address current architecture vulnerabilities, including encrypted gRPC endpoints, compliance with FERPA for student data, automated DynamoDB access controls, and Kubernetes pod security policies to mitigate audit findings.
- **Cost Optimization Tools**: Provide AWS-native cost forecasting, auto-scaling recommendations for Kubernetes clusters, and usage analytics to reduce infrastructure spend that's currently squeezing margins—targeting 20-30% savings.
- **Improved Support and Churn Reduction**: AI-driven ticketing acceleration to slash response times from hours to minutes, integrating with HubSpot/Zapier migrations; predictive churn signals based on support interactions.
- **Hiring and Scaling Efficiency**: Low-code/no-code modules for non-specialized engineers to maintain Go/gRPC pipelines, easing recruitment challenges in a tight EdTech talent market.
- **Scalability for EdTech Workloads**: Handle seasonal spikes (e.g., back-to-school) with horizontal pod autoscaling, DynamoDB global tables for multi-region schools, and gRPC streaming for real-time classroom analytics.
- **Analytics and Reporting**: Custom dashboards overlaying CRM data from HubSpot imports onto Kubernetes metrics, with export to AWS QuickSight.
- **Budget-Aligned Deployment**: Phased rollout fitting ~$450,000 allocation, with options for direct exec sign-off under $200K thresholds.

These requirements stem directly from their pain points, with Amanda Singh prioritizing security and costs in the RFP draft.
