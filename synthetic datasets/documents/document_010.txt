# VaultEdge — Account Overview

## Company Background
VaultEdge is a dynamic fintech company specializing in secure data analytics and compliance solutions for financial institutions, operating with approximately 100 employees across its 51-200 sized organization. Founded in 2018 by a team of former Goldman Sachs engineers frustrated with fragmented data pipelines in traditional banking, the company quickly pivoted to address modern fintech needs, such as real-time risk assessment and automated regulatory reporting. Today, VaultEdge powers transaction monitoring and predictive analytics for mid-tier banks and payment processors, helping them navigate complex regulations like GDPR and SOX while scaling operations efficiently. Their growth has been fueled by a focus on cloud-native architectures, but recent challenges including compliance audit failures on data handling have highlighted vulnerabilities in their legacy systems. As they plan a full migration off these outdated platforms by the end of the year, VaultEdge is prioritizing tools that enhance pipeline visibility and streamline deployments, positioning themselves for aggressive expansion in the competitive fintech SaaS space.

## Technical Requirements
- **Enhanced Pipeline Visibility**: Real-time dashboards and alerting for sales and data pipelines, addressing current poor visibility that blinds their teams to bottlenecks; must integrate natively with HubSpot CRM and Zapier automations for a smooth transition.
- **Compliance and Data Handling**: Automated audit trails and encryption at rest/transit compliant with fintech standards (SOC 2, GDPR), directly tackling recent compliance audit failures that flagged insecure data flows in legacy systems.
- **Accelerated Deployment Cycles**: CI/CD pipelines reducing release times from 2-3 weeks to days, leveraging their Python/FastAPI codebase deployed on AWS Lambda, DynamoDB, and SQS to eliminate manual interventions.
- **Scalability During Peaks**: Auto-scaling serverless architecture to handle traffic spikes without downtime, a critical fix for their reported scaling issues that disrupt operations quarterly.
- **Legacy Migration Support**: Tools for data export/import and hybrid compatibility during their end-of-year migration off legacy platforms, ensuring zero disruption to ongoing Python services.
- **Custom Analytics Integration**: Support for custom Python scripts and FastAPI endpoints, with SQS/DynamoDB hooks for queuing analytics jobs, aligning with their mid-sized 51-200 employee team's agile workflows.
- **POC Environment**: Rapidly deployable sandbox for Chen's analytics team, including sample data mirroring their pipeline health metrics, ready within two weeks to maintain momentum.
