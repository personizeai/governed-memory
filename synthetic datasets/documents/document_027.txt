# HealthSync — Account Overview

## Company Background
HealthSync is a leading healthcare technology company headquartered in San Francisco, California, specializing in the development of integrated platforms for patient data synchronization, electronic health records (EHR) management, and telehealth solutions. With approximately 2,237 employees—firmly placing it in the 1000+ company size category—HealthSync serves a diverse clientele including major hospital networks, outpatient clinics, and independent practices across North America and Europe. The company was founded in 2014 by a visionary team of clinicians and software engineers who had firsthand experience with the chaos of fragmented health data systems during their time at a large urban hospital. Frustrated by delays in critical patient information sharing that often compromised care quality, they bootstrapped the initial product using open-source tools, securing Series A funding within 18 months to scale operations. Today, HealthSync powers seamless data flows for millions of patient interactions annually, leveraging a modern technology stack that includes Rust for high-performance backend services built with the Actix web framework, AWS for cloud infrastructure, PostgreSQL for robust relational data storage, and NATS for real-time event streaming and microservices communication. Despite these cutting-edge components, the organization still relies heavily on a legacy Java application for core support ticketing and deployment orchestration, which is maintained single-handedly by one developer—a bottleneck that exacerbates their operational challenges. This mix of innovation and legacy systems underscores HealthSync's growth trajectory, from a startup disrupting siloed healthcare IT to a mid-sized enterprise navigating scalability pains in a highly regulated industry.

## Technical Requirements
- **Accelerated CI/CD Pipelines**: Need to slash deployment cycles from the current 2-3 weeks per release to under 48 hours, with automated testing, blue-green deployments, and rollback capabilities fully compatible with AWS environments and Rust/Actix services.
- **Support Response Automation**: Implement AI-driven ticketing and resolution tools to combat customer churn caused by slow support times, integrating with PostgreSQL for data persistence and NATS for real-time notifications to agents and customers.
- **Legacy Migration Path**: Seamless integration or gradual replacement for their legacy Java application, currently maintained by a single person, minimizing downtime and knowledge silos during transition.
- **Security and Compliance Features**: Built-in audit logging, encryption at rest/transit, and SOC 2/ HIPAA readiness to prepare for their Q3 2026 security audit, with vulnerability scanning tied to Rust and Actix codebases.
- **Scalability for Tech Stack**: Native support for Rust/Actix web frameworks, AWS orchestration (EKS/ECS), PostgreSQL sharding, and NATS streaming to handle 2,237-employee-scale operations without performance degradation.
- **Analytics Dashboards**: Real-time metrics on deployment success rates, support SLAs, and churn predictors, customizable via PostgreSQL queries and visualized for stakeholders like David Singh.
- **Multi-Tenant Isolation**: Ensure patient data synchronization remains secure across healthcare clients, leveraging AWS IAM and NATS subjects for granular access controls.
- **API-First Integrations**: REST/gRPC endpoints for Actix services, with webhook support for NATS events, enabling quick onboarding of telehealth and EHR modules.
- **Monitoring and Observability**: Integration with Prometheus/Grafana on AWS, alerting on deployment delays or support backlogs to proactively address churn risks.
- **Budget-Aligned Customization**: Features scoped to fit within ~$120,000 annual commitment, prioritizing high-ROI items like deployment speed and support efficiency.
