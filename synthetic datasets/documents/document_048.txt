# CourseForge — Account Overview

## Company Background
CourseForge is a leading EdTech company specializing in AI-powered course creation and delivery platforms, enabling educational institutions and corporations to build, customize, and scale online learning experiences. Founded in 2015 by a team of former Google engineers frustrated with the rigidity of legacy learning management systems (LMS), the company has grown rapidly to approximately 3,232 employees across offices in San Francisco, London, and Bangalore. This expansion was fueled by a Series B round in 2019 that accelerated product development, but it also introduced significant technical debt from rapid early growth, particularly in integrating disparate systems. Today, CourseForge serves over 500 enterprise clients, processing millions of learner interactions daily through its cloud-native platform built on a modern tech stack including Java, Spring Boot for backend services, Azure for hosting, Oracle databases, and Kafka for event streaming. The company's engineering team is in expansion mode, having hired 13 new engineers this quarter to address scaling challenges amid ongoing hiring difficulties for specialized talent.

## Technical Requirements
- **CRM Integration**: Seamless replacement/augmentation for HubSpot CRM + Zapier automations, supporting real-time syncing of sales pipelines with engineering Jira/Confluence data to eliminate silos.
- **Tech Stack Compatibility**: Native support for Java/Spring Boot microservices, Azure DevOps pipelines, Oracle relational data, and Kafka event buses for handling high-volume learner/course metadata.
- **Scalability and Debt Reduction**: Tools to refactor legacy monoliths burdened by technical debt from rapid early growth, including auto-generated APIs and migration paths without downtime.
- **AI-Driven Workflows**: Custom Copilot-like agents for course content generation, integrated with their LMS, outperforming generic HubSpot AI in handling EdTech-specific use cases.
- **Team Collaboration**: Cross-team dashboards breaking data silos between sales (HubSpot metrics) and engineering (Azure/Oracle telemetry), with role-based access.
- **Hiring and Onboarding Support**: Features to accelerate ramp-up for new engineers (e.g., the 13 hired this quarter), such as automated knowledge bases and skill-matching from internal wikis.
- **POC Deliverables**: Two-week technical POC validating end-to-end flows, including Kafka pub/sub for sales-engineering handoffs, by end of March.
- **Security and Compliance**: SOC2 alignment, Azure AD integration, and Oracle data encryption for enterprise EdTech clients.

## Competitive Landscape
- **Existing CRM Workflows**: Heavy investment in HubSpot + Zapier, but custom automations are failing at scale; they're optimizing these internally before full replacement.
- **Microsoft Copilot**: Evaluated for Azure synergy and AI coding assistance, but lacks deep Oracle/Kafka integration for their data silos; POC stalled due to customization limits.
- **HubSpot AI**: Native upsell path, promising workflow automation, yet criticized for shallow EdTech support and inability to bridge sales-engineering gaps amid technical debt.

CourseForge's team views these as short-term patches rather than transformative solutions, giving us an edge in stack-native capabilities. They've deprioritized others post-board shift last month.

## Risk Factors

- **POC Delays or Failures**: Engineering hurdles from technical debt could extend the two-phase process; their difficulty hiring specialized engineers (despite 13 recent adds) strains validation resources.
- **Competitor Resurgence**: Reinvigorated push on HubSpot AI or Copilot updates might sway Emily Kaur if our POC doesn't demonstrably crush silos faster.
