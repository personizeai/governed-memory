# ChannelBridge — Account Overview

## Company Background
ChannelBridge is a dynamic e-commerce platform specializing in multi-channel retail solutions, enabling brands to seamlessly integrate online stores, physical retail, and social commerce channels. With approximately 363 employees, the company falls within the 201-1000 size bracket, positioning it as a mid-sized player in the competitive e-commerce industry. Founded in 2018 by a trio of former Amazon executives frustrated with fragmented customer data across sales channels, ChannelBridge quickly scaled by addressing the pain of siloed inventory and order management. Today, it powers over 500 mid-market retailers, processing millions in transactions annually, but faces growing challenges like scaling during peak traffic periods—such as Black Friday surges—that strain their infrastructure. Their tech stack, built on Rust for high-performance backends with Actix web framework, leverages AWS for cloud hosting, PostgreSQL for reliable data storage, and NATS for real-time messaging, all while planning a full migration off legacy monolithic systems by the end of this year to modernize operations.

## Technical Requirements
- **Scalability for Peak Traffic**: Support for auto-scaling Rust/Actix services on AWS to handle Black Friday-level surges without downtime, addressing current bottlenecks that cause order processing delays.
- **Database and Messaging Integration**: Seamless connectivity with PostgreSQL for transactional data and NATS for real-time pub/sub messaging, enabling low-latency customer interactions.
- **CRM Automation**: Eliminate manual onboarding processes by integrating with or replacing Pipedrive-Slack workflows, automating customer data sync and support ticketing to cut response times and churn.
- **Legacy Migration Support**: Tools for phased migration from monolithic legacy systems by end-of-year, with minimal disruption to existing Rust backend.
- **Cost Optimization**: Features to reduce high AWS infrastructure costs through efficient resource provisioning and monitoring, preserving margins on their ~$450k allocated budget.
- **Real-Time Support Analytics**: Dashboards to track and improve support response times, directly tackling customer churn linked to delays.
- **Multi-Channel E-commerce Sync**: Unified data layer for online, retail, and social channels, leveraging their stack for performant order and inventory management.

These requirements align closely with their 363-employee operation's growth trajectory, where engineering leads like Yusuf emphasize performance in Rust and Actix environments.
