# EduFlow — Account Overview

## Company Background
EduFlow is a dynamic EdTech company specializing in scalable online learning platforms that empower educational institutions and corporate training programs to deliver personalized, interactive courses at scale. With approximately 138 employees—fitting squarely in the 51-200 company size range—their main office is located in the heart of San Francisco's vibrant tech ecosystem, fostering innovation amid the city's competitive startup scene. Founded in 2018 by a trio of former educators and software engineers who grew frustrated with rigid, outdated learning management systems (LMS), EduFlow quickly pivoted from a bootstrapped MVP to a venture-backed leader in adaptive learning tech. Their platform serves over 500K active learners globally, integrating gamification, AI-driven content recommendations, and real-time analytics to boost engagement and outcomes. The company's growth has been fueled by strategic partnerships with universities and Fortune 500 firms, but recent challenges like security vulnerabilities in their aging architecture and rising customer churn due to sluggish support response times have prompted a deeper evaluation of enterprise-grade tools to modernize operations. Built primarily on a modern JavaScript stack including TypeScript, React for the frontend, Node.js for backend services, Google Cloud Platform (GCP) for hosting, and MongoDB for data persistence, EduFlow is poised for expansion but needs solutions that align seamlessly with this ecosystem.

## Technical Requirements
- **Seamless Integration with Existing Stack**: Must support TypeScript for type-safe development, React for dynamic UIs, Node.js for scalable APIs, GCP for cloud-native deployment, and MongoDB for flexible NoSQL data handling—ensuring zero-downtime migrations from their current GCP-centric environment.
- **Enhanced Security Posture**: Address vulnerabilities in their legacy architecture through features like zero-trust access, automated compliance scanning (FERPA/GDPR), and encrypted data flows to mitigate risks that have plagued their San Francisco-based operations.
- **Rapid Support Response Engine**: AI-powered ticketing and automation to slash response times from days to minutes, directly tackling customer churn rates that have climbed due to delayed resolutions in their edtech platform.
- **Legacy System Compatibility**: Robust APIs and middleware for bridging integration complexity with on-prem relics, enabling hybrid cloud strategies without disrupting 138-employee workflows.
- **Scalability for 500K+ Users**: Horizontal scaling on GCP with real-time analytics dashboards, supporting EduFlow's growth from 51-200 headcount while maintaining sub-second query performance on MongoDB clusters.
- **Custom EdTech Workflows**: Gamification hooks, learner analytics, and content personalization modules configurable via low-code interfaces, aligned with their React/Node.js frontend-backend synergy.
- **Budget-Aligned Deployment**: Phased rollout fitting a ~$350K allocation, with quarterly IT committee sign-offs starting Q3 2026.
