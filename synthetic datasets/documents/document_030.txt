# DockSmart — Account Overview

## Company Background

DockSmart is a mid-sized logistics and supply chain management company specializing in intelligent dock scheduling and warehouse optimization solutions for e-commerce and retail sectors. With approximately 786 employees spread across offices in Seattle, Chicago, and Atlanta, the company has established itself as a key player in streamlining last-mile delivery operations amid the booming demand for efficient fulfillment centers. Founded in 2012 by former Amazon logistics executives frustrated with fragmented dock management tools during the early e-commerce surge, DockSmart started as a bootstrapped startup offering basic scheduling software that quickly gained traction with regional distributors. Over the years, it expanded into AI-driven predictive analytics for dock utilization, helping clients reduce wait times by up to 40%. The company's growth has been steady, fueled by strategic acquisitions of two smaller SaaS tools in 2018 and 2021, but it faced a setback last quarter when it lost a major customer—a large grocery chain—due to persistent onboarding issues that highlighted gaps in their legacy systems. Currently, DockSmart is aggressively scaling its engineering team, having hired 19 new developers this quarter alone to support upcoming migrations and product enhancements. This positions them well in a competitive logistics landscape where real-time visibility and automation are paramount.

## Technical Requirements

- **Dock Scheduling Optimization**: Real-time AI-driven scheduling to minimize truck wait times, integrating with existing Ruby on Rails frontend for dynamic UI updates; must handle peak volumes from their 786-employee logistics ops without latency.
- **Legacy System Migration Support**: Tools for seamless data export/import from proprietary legacy platforms, timed for their end-of-year overhaul, including automated mapping for historical dock logs stored in PostgreSQL.
- **Integration with Current Stack**: Native compatibility with Heroku deployment pipelines, PostgreSQL schemas for analytics data, and Sidekiq workers for async processing of scheduling jobs—critical as their engineering team, bolstered by 19 recent hires, builds custom extensions.
- **Analytics and Reporting Dashboard**: Advanced dashboards for Maria Chen's analytics team, featuring predictive modeling for dock utilization; must support direct CEO reporting with exportable visualizations.
- **Security and Compliance Features**: Audit-ready logging and encryption to prepare for Q3 security review, including role-based access aligned with IT directors Alex Bhandari and Anita Nakamura's standards.
- **Scalability for Growth**: Horizontal scaling to accommodate engineering expansion and increased shipment volumes post-major customer loss recovery efforts.
- **Onboarding Automation**: Self-service portals to prevent past onboarding issues that led to client churn last quarter, with guided workflows for customer success teams under Amanda Petrov.
- **API-First Architecture**: Robust RESTful APIs for product-led integrations championed by James Hassan, ensuring extensibility with third-party tools.
- **Custom Workflows**: Support for Sidekiq-based background tasks specific to logistics, like delay notifications and rescheduling queues.
- **Mobile Accessibility**: Responsive apps for warehouse floor use, syncing with Heroku-hosted services in real-time.
