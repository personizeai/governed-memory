# RetailNova — Account Overview

## Company Background

RetailNova is a dynamic e-commerce platform specializing in omnichannel retail solutions for mid-sized fashion and lifestyle brands, operating with approximately 863 employees across its headquarters in San Francisco and regional offices in New York and Austin. Founded in 2012 by former Amazon executives frustrated with the rigidity of existing retail tech stacks, the company bootstrapped its way to profitability by focusing on customizable storefronts that integrate seamlessly with inventory management systems. Today, RetailNova powers online sales for over 500 merchants, handling peak traffic during holiday seasons with a robust backend built primarily on Ruby on Rails, deployed on Heroku, and supported by PostgreSQL for data persistence and Sidekiq for background job processing. The firm's growth has been steady, but it faced a setback last quarter when it lost a major customer due to prolonged onboarding issues, prompting an internal push to modernize their CRM and sales operations tools. Classified as a mid-market player in the 201-1000 employee range, RetailNova continues to innovate in personalized shopping experiences while navigating competitive pressures in the fast-evolving e-commerce landscape. Their commitment to scalability is evident in ongoing plans for a full migration off legacy systems by the end of the year, ensuring they remain agile amid rising customer expectations.

## Technical Requirements

RetailNova's requirements are centered on replacing their aging Salesforce implementation while preserving compatibility with their e-commerce infrastructure. Key needs include:

- **CRM Modernization**: Seamless replacement for Salesforce with custom Apex triggers, supporting sales pipelines for a 201-1000 employee organization handling high-volume e-commerce transactions.
- **Integration with Existing Stack**: Native compatibility with Ruby on Rails applications deployed on Heroku, PostgreSQL for scalable data storage, and Sidekiq for reliable background processing to avoid disruptions during peak retail seasons.
- **Migration Support**: Tools and services to facilitate a complete migration from legacy systems by the end of the year, minimizing downtime for their 863-employee operations.
- **Security and Compliance**: Advanced auditing features to prepare for their Q3 security audit, including role-based access controls, data encryption, and audit logging tailored to e-commerce PCI compliance.
- **Scalability for Growth**: Handling increased loads post their recent customer loss, with analytics dashboards for sales teams led by Brandon Alvarez to monitor performance metrics.
- **Customization and Extensibility**: Low-code options for custom workflows, reducing reliance on engineering for the VP of Sales and Head of Product teams.
- **Onboarding Efficiency**: Accelerated setup processes to prevent repeats of last quarter's major customer churn due to onboarding issues.
- **Reporting and Analytics**: Real-time insights into sales funnels, integrated with their PostgreSQL data warehouse for the Head of Data's team.
- **Budget Alignment**: Solutions fitting within the ~$850k allocation, with phased rollout to match medium urgency levels.

These requirements reflect a strategic shift toward agility, with the tech stack reiterated as a non-negotiable foundation.

## Competitive Landscape

RetailNova is evaluating several alternatives in parallel to our solution, reflecting a thorough procurement approach typical for their e-commerce scale:

- **HubSpot**: Strong contender for inbound sales alignment, but concerns over deep Ruby on Rails integrations and Heroku deployment compatibility have been voiced.
- **Pipedrive**: Attractive for its simplicity and sales focus, appealing to Brandon's team; however, lacks robust data handling for PostgreSQL/Sidekiq workflows.
- **Zoho CRM**: Budget-friendly option under their $850k cap, but customization falls short of Salesforce Apex replacements and migration support.
- **Freshsales**: Gaining traction for AI features, yet their Q3 security audit readiness is unproven in RetailNova's stack.

We differentiate through precise e-commerce integrations and onboarding speed, critical after their recent churn. No single competitor dominates, giving us an edge in technical deep dives.
