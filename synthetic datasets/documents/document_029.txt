# MetalGrid — Account Overview

## Company Background
MetalGrid is a mid-sized manufacturing company specializing in the production of precision metal components and assemblies for the aerospace, automotive, and renewable energy sectors. With approximately 506 employees and annual revenue hovering around $157 million, the company has carved out a niche in high-volume, custom fabrication using advanced CNC machining and automated welding processes. Founded in 2012 by a trio of mechanical engineers—former employees of a larger industrial conglomerate—who identified a gap in the market for agile, on-demand metal grid structures critical for battery enclosures and structural supports in electric vehicles, MetalGrid quickly scaled from a garage operation in Seattle to a 150,000 sq ft facility in Kent, Washington. Their growth trajectory has been fueled by strategic partnerships with Tier 1 suppliers like Boeing and Tesla, but recent expansions have introduced challenges such as technical debt from rapid early growth. Today, they operate in a competitive landscape where efficiency is paramount, and they're actively addressing high infrastructure costs eating into margins through cloud optimization strategies.

## Technical Requirements
- **Cloud Infrastructure Optimization**: Support for Azure environments to address high infrastructure costs eating into margins, with automated cost allocation and rightsizing recommendations for AKS clusters and VM instances.
- **Application Monitoring for Java/Spring Boot**: Full observability into microservices, including distributed tracing, metrics, and logging to tackle technical debt from rapid early growth and enable better ROI measurement on AI initiatives.
- **Database Integration with Oracle**: Real-time performance monitoring and query optimization tools to support legacy migration by end of year, ensuring minimal downtime during cutover.
- **Event Streaming with Kafka**: End-to-end visibility into topics, consumer lag, and throughput to handle IoT data from manufacturing lines, scaling with the engineering team's growth (12 hires this quarter).
- **AI/ML Observability**: Custom dashboards for tracking model performance, inference costs, and business impact, directly addressing difficulties in measuring ROI on AI initiatives like predictive maintenance.
- **Sales Team Alignment Tools**: Reporting features to resolve inconsistent messaging across sales, integrating with CRM for unified customer views.
- **POC Environment Setup**: Isolated sandbox for Anita's team to evaluate integration with their stack (Java, Spring Boot, Azure, Oracle, Kafka) without production risk.
- **Scalability for Company Size**: Handle workloads for a 506-employee manufacturing firm with $157M revenue, supporting growth without proportional cost increases.

These requirements stem from their manufacturing operations, where precision in metal grid production demands reliable, cost-effective tech infrastructure.

## Competitive Landscape
MetalGrid is evaluating a shortlist of three competitors alongside our solution:
- **Datadog**: Strong in Azure and Kafka monitoring, but their pricing exceeds the $50K budget and lacks deep Oracle integration, which Anita cited as a non-starter.
- **New Relic**: Appeals to Chloe for Java/Spring Boot telemetry, yet they worry about its complexity for a growing engineering team, and it doesn't fully address AI ROI measurement.
- **Dynatrace**: Positioned well for full-stack observability, including AI causation, but recent demos revealed gaps in manufacturing-specific IoT handling for Kafka streams, plus higher TCO impacting margins.

We've differentiated on cost-effectiveness for their 201-1000 employee scale and seamless POC setup, positioning us as the pragmatic choice amid their technical debt and migration pressures. Priya mentioned last week that Datadog is the frontrunner internally, but our focus on pain points like inconsistent sales messaging has gained traction.
