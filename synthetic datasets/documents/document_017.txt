# CloudNova — Account Overview

## Company Background
CloudNova is a B2B SaaS company headquartered in San Francisco, specializing in AI-driven workflow automation tools for mid-sized enterprises in the tech and finance sectors. With a lean team of about 37 employees—fitting squarely within the 11-50 employee size bracket—the company has carved out a niche by helping businesses streamline complex processes like contract management and compliance tracking through intuitive, scalable platforms. Founded in 2018 by a trio of former Google engineers, Tariq Mueller (now VP of Engineering), along with siblings Elena and Rajiv Patel, CloudNova emerged from frustration with rigid, legacy on-premise systems that hampered agility during their time at larger firms. The founders bootstrapped the initial product using open-source technologies, launching their flagship platform, NovaFlow, which quickly gained traction among startups scaling operations. Over the past five years, CloudNova has grown steadily, securing Series A funding in 2021 and expanding its customer base to over 200 active clients. However, challenges persist: last quarter, they lost a major customer—a Fortune 500 financial services firm—due to protracted onboarding issues stemming from integration bottlenecks with their aging infrastructure. This setback has accelerated internal discussions around modernization, including a planned migration off legacy systems by the end of the year. Bolstering these efforts, their IT budget was recently increased by 17% this fiscal year, signaling strong executive commitment to tech investments amid competitive pressures in the SaaS space.

## Technical Requirements
- **Legacy Migration Support**: Full assistance for transitioning off on-premise legacy systems by December 2026, including data export tools compatible with MongoDB schemas and minimal downtime orchestration to prevent repeats of recent customer onboarding failures.
- **GCP Integration**: Native compatibility with Google Cloud Platform for hosting and scaling, ensuring seamless deployment alongside their Node.js microservices architecture.
- **Frontend/Backend Alignment**: Robust SDKs for TypeScript and React applications, with hooks and components that accelerate development cycles for their 37-employee engineering team.
- **Database Handling**: Optimized connectors for MongoDB, supporting high-volume reads/writes during peak SaaS usage, with built-in sharding and replication mirroring their current setup.
- **Scalability for Growth**: Auto-scaling features to handle expansion from 11-50 employees, incorporating AI-driven resource allocation tied to their 17% IT budget increase.
- **Security and Compliance**: SOC 2 Type II compliance, with role-based access controls (RBAC) integrable via Node.js middleware, addressing finance sector client needs post-churn incident.
- **Analytics Dashboards**: Customizable React-based dashboards for marketing teams (Chloe and Lisa), pulling real-time data from GCP/MongoDB for campaign ROI tracking.
- **API-First Design**: RESTful and GraphQL endpoints fully supporting their TypeScript ecosystem, with webhooks for event-driven workflows.
- **Onboarding Acceleration**: Automated setup wizards and migration playbooks to mitigate risks from past quarter's customer loss.
- **Budget-Aligned Customization**: Flexible pricing tiers leveraging their fiscal year IT boost, with PoC environments for quick validation by end users like Simone.
