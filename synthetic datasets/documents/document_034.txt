# CoinTrail — Account Overview

## Company Background

CoinTrail is a prominent Fintech company headquartered in Denver, Colorado, specializing in blockchain analytics and cryptocurrency transaction tracing solutions for financial institutions and regulatory bodies. With approximately 562 employees—falling squarely within the 201-1000 company size range—the organization has established itself as a key player in the digital asset compliance space. Founded in 2015 by a group of former Wall Street analysts and blockchain engineers who identified gaps in real-time transaction monitoring during the early crypto boom, CoinTrail quickly pivoted from a startup consultancy to a full-scale SaaS platform provider. Their growth trajectory accelerated post-2020 with the surge in institutional crypto adoption, leading to expansions in engineering and data teams. Notably, their engineering team is growing rapidly, having hired 5 people this quarter alone to bolster serverless architectures amid increasing demand. The company's tech stack heavily features Python for backend logic, FastAPI for API development, AWS Lambda for scalable compute, DynamoDB for NoSQL storage, and SQS for message queuing, enabling them to handle high-volume transaction data efficiently. This setup underscores their commitment to cloud-native, event-driven systems tailored for Fintech scalability. However, recent challenges, such as losing a major customer last quarter due to onboarding issues, have heightened internal focus on streamlining customer acquisition processes.

## Technical Requirements

- **Scalable Integration with Existing CRM**: Must support Salesforce with custom Apex triggers for bidirectional syncing without refactoring legacy code; current solution causes delays in transaction pipeline updates.
- **Serverless Compatibility**: Full compatibility with Python, FastAPI APIs, AWS Lambda functions, DynamoDB for data persistence, and SQS for asynchronous messaging to handle 10M+ daily crypto traces.
- **Real-Time Onboarding Workflows**: Automated, low-touch onboarding to prevent repeats of last quarter's major customer loss due to manual processes; requires AI-driven validation and compliance checks.
- **Event-Driven Architecture Support**: Native handling of high-throughput events with sub-second latency, integrating into their Denver-based infrastructure without vendor-specific dependencies.
- **Multi-Vendor Evaluation Alignment**: Solution must differentiate in POCs against four competitors, providing superior audit trails and customization via no-code tools.
- **Engineering Team Scalability**: Tools to empower their recently expanded team (5 new hires this quarter) with drag-and-drop integrations and monitoring dashboards.
- **Compliance and Security**: SOC 2 Type II, GDPR-ready features for Fintech, with granular access controls mirroring their DynamoDB/SQS permissions model.
- **Deployment Timeline Fit**: Quick-setup options to meet the July 28, 2026, decision timeline, including sandbox environments for internal demos.

Their tech stack—Python, FastAPI, AWS Lambda, DynamoDB, and SQS—demands a vendor-agnostic orchestrator that enhances rather than replaces these components.
