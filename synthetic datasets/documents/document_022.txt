# ShipGrid — Account Overview

## Company Background
ShipGrid is a Chicago-based logistics technology company specializing in supply chain optimization software for mid-sized e-commerce and retail businesses. With approximately 93 employees, primarily operating out of their main office in Chicago, the company helps clients streamline shipping operations, predict demand fluctuations, and reduce fulfillment costs through an integrated platform. Founded in 2018 by former UPS executives who identified gaps in real-time visibility for last-mile delivery during the e-commerce boom, ShipGrid quickly scaled from a bootstrapped startup to a key player in the logistics SaaS space. Their growth has been fueled by partnerships with major carriers and a focus on data-driven routing algorithms, but recent expansion has highlighted internal challenges like scaling issues during peak traffic periods when their platform handles surges from holiday shopping rushes. Today, as a company in the 51-200 employee range, ShipGrid continues to innovate in a competitive logistics market, serving clients across North America.

## Technical Requirements
- **Scalability During Peaks**: Solution must handle 10x traffic surges during holiday seasons without latency >500ms, integrating seamlessly with GCP autoscaling groups currently strained by Node.js workloads.
- **Data Silo Integration**: Unify datasets from engineering (MongoDB-stored operational logs) and sales (React dashboard exports), enabling real-time querying to eliminate silos that currently delay pipeline health reporting.
- **Pipeline Visibility Dashboards**: Custom React-based UI components for visualizing sales funnel metrics, with TypeScript type safety to match their frontend stack and drill-downs into logistics-specific KPIs like delivery ETAs.
- **GCP-Native Deployment**: Full support for Google Cloud Platform services, including Kubernetes orchestration for Node.js microservices, to avoid vendor lock-in while enhancing peak-period resilience.
- **MongoDB Compatibility**: Native connectors for schema migrations and aggregations, addressing current issues where sales queries overload engineering databases during high-volume periods.
- **API-First Architecture**: RESTful endpoints with WebSocket support for live updates on shipping pipelines, reducing the poor visibility that plagues their current setup.
- **Security and Compliance**: SOC 2 Type II alignment for logistics data handling, with role-based access to prevent unauthorized cross-team data exposure from silos.
- **Custom Analytics Layer**: ML-driven forecasting for peak traffic prediction, layered atop their existing stack to provide actionable insights into pipeline bottlenecks.
