# ChannelBridge — Account Overview

## Company Background
ChannelBridge is a dynamic e-commerce platform specializing in multi-channel retail solutions that bridge online marketplaces, physical stores, and social commerce channels for mid-sized retailers worldwide. With approximately 333 employees, fitting squarely into the 201-1000 company size bracket, the organization has carved out a niche by enabling seamless inventory synchronization, personalized customer experiences, and real-time analytics across disparate sales channels. Founded in 2018 by former Amazon executives Sarah Lin and Marcus Patel, who identified a critical gap in affordable, scalable tools for cross-border e-commerce during their time managing high-volume logistics, ChannelBridge bootstrapped its way to profitability within three years before raising a Series B round in 2022 to fuel international expansion. Today, it powers over 5,000 merchants across North America and Europe, processing millions in transactions monthly, but grapples with scaling issues during peak traffic periods—such as Black Friday surges—that strain its infrastructure. High infrastructure costs on Google Cloud Platform (GCP) are also eating into margins, compounded by technical debt from rapid early growth and the difficulty in hiring specialized engineers amid a booming engineering team that just hired 12 people this quarter. The company is aggressively planning a migration off legacy systems by the end of the year to modernize its stack, which currently relies heavily on Java, Micronaut frameworks, GCP services including Spanner for database management and Pub/Sub for messaging.

## Technical Requirements
- **Peak Traffic Scaling**: Real-time auto-scaling capabilities to handle Black Friday-level surges without Pub/Sub backlogs or Spanner throttling, targeting <100ms p99 latencies during 10x traffic spikes—critical given their ongoing scaling issues.
- **Infrastructure Cost Optimization**: Native GCP integrations for cost forecasting, rightsizing recommendations, and reserved instance management to curb bills eating into margins, with ROI projections showing 25-35% savings.
- **Tech Stack Compatibility**: Seamless support for Java and Micronaut microservices, including instrumentation for Spanner queries and Pub/Sub event streams, to accelerate their engineering team's output post the 12 hires this quarter.
- **Legacy Migration Support**: Tools for phased cutover from legacy monoliths by end-of-year, including data synchronization, schema migration for Spanner, and zero-downtime deployment pipelines to mitigate technical debt.
- **Hiring and Team Enablement**: Analytics dashboards for resource allocation and skill gap identification, addressing difficulty hiring specialized engineers in a competitive market.
- **Monitoring and Alerting**: Unified observability across GCP services with custom anomaly detection for e-commerce metrics like conversion funnels and inventory syncs.
- **Security and Compliance**: SOC2-ready features with fine-grained access controls for their multi-tenant retailer platform, ensuring GDPR/CCPA adherence during channel bridging.
- **API-First Integrations**: REST/gRPC endpoints compatible with their stack, plus webhooks for real-time updates to minimize custom dev work.
