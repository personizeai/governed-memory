# ContentHQ — Account Overview

## Company Background
ContentHQ is a dynamic digital media company specializing in content creation, distribution, and analytics platforms for publishers and brands worldwide. With approximately 440 employees—falling squarely in the 201-1000 company size range—the organization operates primarily from its main office in London, UK, with satellite teams in New York and Singapore. Founded in 2015 by a group of former BBC executives frustrated with legacy content management systems, ContentHQ quickly pivoted to a cloud-native SaaS model, emphasizing real-time personalization and AI-driven recommendations to help media outlets monetize their audiences more effectively. The company has grown steadily through acquisitions of smaller analytics startups, but faced a setback last quarter when they lost a major customer due to protracted onboarding issues that exposed gaps in their internal CRM and sales enablement tools. This incident has heightened their focus on seamless integration solutions. Additionally, a recent board meeting last month prompted a strategic shift in priorities, moving away from expansive global expansion toward optimizing core operations and technology infrastructure to regain momentum.

## Technical Requirements
- **Seamless Integration with Legacy Stack**: Must support PHP/Laravel APIs, DigitalOcean deployments, MySQL databases, and Redis caching layers without requiring middleware rewrites; critical for their content delivery network handling 10M+ daily interactions.
- **Real-Time Conversation Intelligence**: AI-powered transcription, sentiment analysis, and deal risk scoring integrated into sales workflows, with sub-2-second latency to match Redis caching speeds.
- **Custom Onboarding Automation**: No-code workflows to eliminate past onboarding pitfalls that led to customer loss; includes automated user provisioning via OAuth and SCIM for their 440-person team.
- **Scalable Analytics Dashboard**: MySQL-backed reporting with Laravel-friendly endpoints, supporting custom KPIs like content engagement-to-conversion ratios and sales velocity metrics.
- **Data Privacy Compliance**: GDPR-ready with London-based data residency options, including audit logs exportable to DigitalOcean object storage.
- **Mobile and Desktop Coaching**: In-call prompts and post-call summaries optimized for remote sales teams across London, NY, and Singapore time zones.
- **API Rate Limiting and Throttling**: Redis-integrated to handle peak loads from media campaigns without downtime.
- **Extensibility for Future AI**: Hooks for custom ML models trained on their proprietary content datasets.
