# CourseForge — Account Overview

## Company Background
CourseForge is a dynamic EdTech company headquartered in Boston, specializing in the development of customizable online course platforms that empower educators and organizations to create, deploy, and manage interactive learning experiences. With approximately 37 employees—falling squarely within the 11-50 size range—they focus on bridging the gap between traditional e-learning tools and modern, AI-enhanced content delivery systems. Founded in 2018 by a trio of MIT alumni frustrated with rigid legacy platforms during their own online teaching ventures amid the rise of remote education, CourseForge bootstrapped its way to profitability by 2022, emphasizing scalable solutions for K-12 schools, corporate training programs, and higher education institutions. Today, they serve over 500 clients globally, but their growth has been hampered by internal challenges like manual processes for customer onboarding and a push to migrate off legacy systems by the end of the year. Their tech stack, built on PHP and Laravel hosted on DigitalOcean with MySQL and Redis for caching and data management, reflects a lean, developer-friendly setup that's now straining under increased demand.

## Technical Requirements
- **Onboarding Automation**: Full elimination of manual processes for customer onboarding, integrating natively with HubSpot CRM and Zapier automations to streamline user provisioning from lead to active learner in under 5 minutes.
- **AI ROI Analytics**: Advanced dashboards for measuring ROI on AI initiatives, including attribution models for personalized course recommendations, engagement uplift metrics, and cost-per-learner breakdowns, addressing their core difficulty in quantifying value.
- **Legacy Migration Support**: Tools for seamless data transfer from legacy systems by end-of-year deadline, with zero-downtime scripts tailored to PHP/Laravel environments on DigitalOcean, ensuring MySQL schema compatibility and Redis session continuity.
- **Scalability for Growth**: Horizontal scaling capabilities to support their 37-employee team's expansion ambitions, handling 10x course enrollment spikes without performance degradation.
- **Reporting and Insights**: Real-time analytics on learner progress, dropout rates, and revenue attribution, integrated with existing MySQL/Redis stack to overcome current HubSpot limitations.
- **Security and Compliance**: SOC 2 Type II compliance, GDPR-ready data handling, and role-based access controls customized for EdTech use cases like multi-tenant course management.
- **Custom Integrations**: API-first design for PHP/Laravel hooks, DigitalOcean droplet orchestration, and Zapier extensibility to future-proof against their evolving stack.
- **Performance Optimization**: Redis-optimized caching for high-traffic course pages and MySQL query tuning to reduce latency in AI-driven personalization.

These requirements directly map to their pain points, with our POC validating 90% out-of-box fit last month.
