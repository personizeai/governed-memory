# CareStack — Account Overview

## Company Background

CareStack is a prominent Healthcare Tech company specializing in cloud-based practice management software for dental practices. With approximately 3033 employees—firmly in the 1000+ company size category—and annual revenue hovering around $58M, they have established themselves as a key player in streamlining administrative and clinical workflows for over 5,000 dental offices across North America. Founded in 2015 by a group of practicing dentists and software engineers who were frustrated with fragmented legacy systems that hindered efficient patient care and billing, CareStack quickly scaled from a bootstrapped startup in Florida to a venture-backed leader in the space. Their platform integrates scheduling, billing, patient engagement, and analytics into a single intuitive dashboard, addressing pain points that have plagued the industry for decades. Recently, they've been navigating internal shifts, including a board meeting last month that realigned priorities toward aggressive modernization efforts. This comes amid challenges like losing a major customer last quarter due to onboarding issues, which has heightened their focus on seamless implementation. As they plan a full migration off legacy systems by the end of the year, CareStack's engineering team is growing rapidly—they hired 17 people this quarter to bolster development capacity.

## Technical Requirements

- **Seamless Integration with Existing Stack**: Must integrate natively with dental practice management APIs, replacing their mix of Google Sheets and custom dashboards without data loss during the planned end-of-year migration off legacy systems.
- **Scalable Account Overviews**: Real-time dashboards for customer accounts, supporting 3033-employee scale and handling increased load from engineering team's growth (17 hires this quarter).
- **Onboarding Automation**: Advanced workflows to prevent issues like those that caused loss of a major customer last quarter, including self-service portals and AI-driven setup.
- **Analytics and Reporting**: Customizable reports for revenue tracking (~$58M ARR visibility), with export to tools like Google Sheets for legacy compatibility during transition.
- **Multi-User Access Controls**: Role-based permissions for key contacts like VP of Customer Success (Maria Kaur, reporting to CEO), CRO (budget owner), engineering director (end user), and marketing VP (champion).
- **Compliance and Security**: HIPAA-compliant features essential for Healthcare Tech, with audit trails for board-reviewed priorities shifted last month.
- **Mobile and API-First Design**: Support for remote teams, given recent engineering expansion and medium urgency level.
- **Budget-Aligned Pricing**: Solutions fitting ~$120,000 annual allocation, with flexible tiers for proposal stage evaluation.
- **RFP Compatibility**: Must address procurement's three-bid requirement, with detailed SLAs for decision timeline of 2026-05-13.

These requirements stem directly from pain points voiced in meetings, particularly around legacy migration and onboarding reliability.

## Competitive Landscape

CareStack is evaluating a competitive set as part of their procurement-mandated three-bid process:

- **Dentrix (Henry Schein)**: Legacy leader in dental software; they're the incumbent for some modules but criticized for poor cloud scalability. CareStack's planned legacy migration makes this a status quo option they're actively looking to escape.
- **OpenDental**: Open-source alternative with strong customization, appealing to their engineering team (recently bolstered by 17 hires this quarter). However, it lacks enterprise-grade support, echoing onboarding issues that cost them a major customer last quarter.
- **Eaglesoft (Patterson Dental)**: Another entrenched player; robust for billing but rigid dashboards don't match their need to replace Google Sheets/custom setups amid $58M revenue growth.
- **Curve Dental**: Cloud-native competitor with similar practice management focus; positioned as a direct threat due to faster onboarding demos, but weaker analytics for their 3033-employee scale.

Our differentiation lies in AI-driven account overviews and migration tooling, which align with post-board priority shifts from last month. Maria's RFP will clarify weighting.
