# RouteWise — Account Overview

## Company Background

RouteWise is a logistics technology company specializing in route optimization and supply chain management software, headquartered in their main office in Seattle, Washington. With approximately 83 employees—falling within the 51-200 company size range—they provide SaaS solutions that help mid-sized logistics firms streamline delivery operations, reduce fuel costs, and improve real-time tracking for fleets across North America. Founded in 2018 by a group of former Amazon logistics engineers frustrated with inefficient routing tools during the e-commerce boom, RouteWise bootstrapped its way to profitability by 2022, focusing on AI-driven algorithms that integrate seamlessly with existing warehouse management systems. Their growth has been steady, fueled by a 23% increase in IT budget this fiscal year, allowing them to tackle internal challenges like fragmented tooling across departments while serving clients in e-commerce fulfillment and last-mile delivery sectors. Today, they handle over 500,000 routes daily for partners like regional grocers and parcel services, positioning them as a nimble player in a competitive logistics SaaS market dominated by giants.

## Technical Requirements

- **API Integration**: Native support for gRPC protocols to interface with their Go-based microservices without middleware overhead, ensuring low-latency route data syncing.
- **Data Unification**: Tools to break down data silos between engineering (DynamoDB-stored telemetry) and sales (CRM exports), enabling cross-team dashboards for route optimization metrics.
- **Kubernetes Compatibility**: Helm charts or operators for seamless deployment on their AWS EKS clusters, supporting auto-scaling for peak logistics seasons.
- **Developer Experience**: IDE plugins and CLI tools tailored for Go developers to improve workflow velocity, addressing fragmented tooling pains with unified CI/CD pipelines.
- **Scalability and Storage**: Horizontal scaling to handle DynamoDB throughput for 500k+ daily routes, with cost-optimized AWS integrations.
- **Security and Compliance**: SOC 2 Type II readiness for their Q3 audit, including RBAC, encryption at rest/transit matching their stack.
- **Analytics Layer**: Real-time querying across silos for sales forecasting and engineering insights, reducing manual ETL processes.
- **Customization**: Extensible via Go SDK for custom logistics algorithms, preserving their IP while enhancing feature rollout speed.

These map directly to their pain points: fragmented tooling, data silos, and poor developer experience slowing feature velocity.

## Competitive Landscape

RouteWise is actively evaluating alternatives to address their fragmented tooling and data silos:
- **ZoomInfo**: Strong contender for sales intelligence, but lacks deep engineering integrations with Kubernetes or gRPC; they're testing it for CRM enrichment, though James noted integration friction with their AWS setup.
- **Intercom**: In play for customer-facing comms and basic analytics, appealing to Lucia's sales team, but falls short on backend unification for DynamoDB route data—seen as a partial fit for silos but not developer velocity.

No mentions of others like HubSpot or Salesforce, but their focus remains on tools bridging eng-sales divides. We've highlighted our logistics-specific edge (e.g., route viz) over these generalists; last month's deep dive positioned us favorably, but they plan side-by-side pilots soon.
