# RouteWise — Account Overview

## Company Background

RouteWise is a mid-sized logistics and supply chain management company operating in the transportation and distribution sector, with approximately 638 employees fitting squarely within the 201-1000 company size range. Founded in 2012 by a group of former UPS executives who identified gaps in real-time route optimization and fleet management software, the company has grown rapidly by providing SaaS-based solutions for last-mile delivery, warehouse automation, and predictive analytics for carriers. Headquartered in Austin, Texas, RouteWise serves over 500 enterprise clients across North America, focusing on e-commerce fulfillment and grocery delivery networks. Their core platform integrates IoT sensors with AI-driven dispatching, helping clients reduce fuel costs by up to 25% and improve on-time delivery rates. Currently, they rely heavily on spreadsheets and manual data entry for internal route planning and sales forecasting, which exacerbates issues like fragmented tooling across departments. The company's technology stack is modern and cloud-native, built primarily on Python with FastAPI for backend APIs, AWS Lambda for serverless functions, DynamoDB for NoSQL storage, and SQS for message queuing—though this setup has led to vendor lock-in with their current cloud provider. A recent board meeting last month shifted priorities toward operational efficiency and security hardening ahead of an upcoming Q2 security audit, underscoring the critical urgency level for modernizing their tooling.

## Technical Requirements

- **Real-time Route Optimization Integration**: Must ingest live telemetry from IoT devices and sync with existing Python/FastAPI services on AWS Lambda, eliminating manual spreadsheet updates for dispatch planning.
- **Scalable Data Storage and Queuing**: Compatibility with DynamoDB for NoSQL logistics datasets and SQS for event-driven workflows; support for multi-cloud egress to mitigate vendor lock-in risks.
- **Cross-Departmental Tool Unification**: Single pane of glass dashboard replacing fragmented tooling across sales (led by Raj Kaur), engineering (Sarah Anderson), and infrastructure (Simone Reyes) teams.
- **Security and Compliance Features**: Audit-ready logging, encryption at rest/transit, and SOC 2 Type II alignment for Q2 security audit; zero-trust access controls for 638-employee organization.
- **API-First Extensibility**: RESTful endpoints with Python SDK for custom FastAPI plugins, ensuring seamless upgrade from current manual data entry processes.
- **High Availability and Performance**: Serverless scaling to handle peak logistics volumes (e.g., holiday surges), with <100ms latency on SQS-triggered updates.
- **Reporting and Analytics**: Advanced dashboards for sales forecasting, reducing reliance on spreadsheets; ML-driven predictions integrated into existing stack.
- **Migration Support**: Phased rollout tools to transition from legacy manual processes without downtime, addressing board-mandated efficiency gains.

These requirements stem directly from their tech stack and pain points, with a critical urgency to deploy before Q2.

## Competitive Landscape

RouteWise is evaluating a shortlist of three alternatives alongside our solution:

- **OptiRoute Pro**: Incumbent for basic routing; strong in spreadsheets integration but lacks AWS-native scalability and Python/FastAPI support, criticized for exacerbating vendor lock-in.
- **LogiFlow Enterprise**: Cloud-agnostic platform with DynamoDB compatibility; appealing for fragmented tooling unification but weaker on real-time SQS queuing and Q2 audit readiness.
- **FleetSync AI**: Emerging player with ML focus; budget-competitive under $1.5M but unproven at RouteWise's 638-employee scale, with manual data entry migration tools seen as inadequate.

Our edge lies in seamless tech stack alignment (Python, FastAPI, Lambda, DynamoDB, SQS) and addressing core pain points like departmental fragmentation and cloud lock-in, bolstered by Raj's 6-year tenure championing modernization.
