# PixelGrid — Account Overview

## Company Background
PixelGrid is a dynamic digital media company specializing in real-time personalized advertising platforms for e-commerce and content publishers. With approximately 45 employees (falling within the 11-50 size range), the company focuses on leveraging big data analytics to deliver hyper-targeted ad experiences that maximize engagement and revenue during high-traffic events. Founded in early 2022 by a trio of former Google engineers who identified a gap in scalable, low-latency ad serving amid the explosion of streaming media, PixelGrid experienced explosive early growth, onboarding over 200 publisher clients in its first 18 months. This rapid expansion, however, has left them grappling with significant technical debt from hasty implementations, including outdated monolithic services that struggle under load. Compounding these challenges, they lost a major customer last quarter due to protracted onboarding issues stemming from their legacy CRM setup, which highlighted broader operational inefficiencies. Recently, their engineering team has been growing aggressively—they hired 17 people this quarter alone—to address these pain points, signaling a commitment to modernization despite the company's relatively lean size.

## Technical Requirements
- **Scalability for Peak Traffic**: Must handle 10x traffic surges (e.g., Black Friday events) with sub-200ms latency, integrating seamlessly with existing Scala/Spark services to mitigate current bottlenecks observed during high-load periods.
- **Data Pipeline Orchestration**: Airflow-compatible scheduling with enhanced monitoring; support for Databricks ML jobs and Snowflake querying without introducing new vendor lock-in.
- **CRM Integration**: Replace or augment Pipedrive + Slack notifications with advanced workflow automation, including AI-driven lead scoring and onboarding sequences to prevent repeats of last quarter's customer loss.
- **Technical Debt Reduction**: Modular architecture to refactor legacy Scala monoliths, enabling faster iterations amid engineering team's recent growth (17 hires this quarter).
- **AI/ML Enhancements**: Copilot-like features for ops and sales, but with custom scalability beyond off-the-shelf tools like Microsoft Copilot or Drift.
- **POC-Ready Customization**: Two-phase support—technical validation first (e.g., Spark job acceleration), then business ROI modeling for leadership.
- **Cloud Agnosticism**: Escape current provider lock-in via portable APIs, ensuring Snowflake/Databricks continuity.
- **Real-Time Analytics**: Spark-streaming integrations for ad personalization, addressing revenue leaks from scaling failures.
- **Security & Compliance**: SOC2 alignment for media client data, with Airflow DAG auditing.
- **Budget Alignment**: Solution scoped to ~$450K annual commitment, with phased rollout.

## Competitive Landscape
- **Building In-House**: Primary threat; engineering expansion (17 hires this quarter) gives them resources to develop custom Scala/Spark tools, though technical debt and onboarding failures (e.g., last quarter's loss) make this risky.
- **Drift**: Evaluating for conversational CRM to replace Pipedrive + Slack, but lacks deep Databricks/Snowflake integration for their ad-scale needs.
- **Microsoft Copilot**: Attracted by AI ops features, yet concerns over vendor lock-in mirror their current cloud pains; POC will differentiate our stack-agnostic approach (Scala/Spark/Airflow/etc.).

## Risk Factors
- **Internal Prioritization Shifts**: Recent engineering hires (17 this quarter) could divert focus from POC to fire-fighting scaling issues, delaying the November 18 timeline; they've hinted at stretching resources thin.
- **Competitor Momentum**: Strong pull toward in-house build or Copilot if our POC doesn't clearly outperform on peak traffic handling—Elena noted "they" (unclear if execs or engineers) favor familiarity.
- **Budget Reallocation**: $450K pot vulnerable if Q3 results echo last quarter's churn; vendor lock-in fears could pivot them to cheaper Drift alternatives.
