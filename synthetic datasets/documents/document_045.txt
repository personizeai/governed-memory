# CastPoint — Account Overview

## Company Background

CastPoint is a prominent manufacturing company specializing in precision-engineered components for the automotive and aerospace sectors, with a workforce of approximately 640 employees spread across its primary operations in London and satellite facilities in Eastern Europe. Founded in 2012 by a trio of mechanical engineers—David Hargrove, Lena Kowalski, and Marcus Finch—who met while working at a legacy auto parts supplier, the company quickly carved out a niche by leveraging advanced CNC machining and additive manufacturing techniques to deliver high-tolerance parts at competitive prices. Starting from a modest 50-person shop in London's industrial outskirts, CastPoint scaled rapidly during the EV boom of the mid-2010s, securing major contracts with OEMs like Jaguar Land Rover and Airbus suppliers. Today, it boasts annual revenues of around $83 million, driven by a 25% CAGR over the past five years, though recent supply chain disruptions have tempered growth to single digits. The company's main office in London serves as the hub for executive leadership, R&D, and sales, while production is optimized for just-in-time delivery to European clients. CastPoint's commitment to innovation is evident in its adoption of modern web technologies for internal tools, including a TypeScript, React, Node.js stack hosted on GCP with MongoDB for data persistence, reflecting a pivot from legacy on-prem systems about three years ago. However, this shift has introduced challenges like vendor lock-in with their current cloud provider, which leadership is keen to address.

This 90-minute session involved Raj Chen, Emily Patel, and our engineering leads, diving into their tech stack: TypeScript for type-safe development, React for dynamic UIs, Node.js for scalable services, GCP for hosting, and MongoDB for flexible schema handling. ## Technical Requirements

- **Unified Data Access**: Break down silos between engineering (MongoDB production logs on GCP) and sales teams (CRM silos), enabling real-time dashboards queryable via TypeScript/React apps; must support federated queries without data duplication.
- **Security Enhancements**: Address vulnerabilities in current Node.js/GCP architecture, including automated vuln scanning, zero-trust API gateways, and compliance with ISO 27001—critical after recent arch reviews flagged exposed endpoints.
- **Cloud Agnosticism**: Mitigate GCP vendor lock-in with portable data pipelines; support seamless migration to alternatives like AWS or Azure, preserving MongoDB schemas and React frontend integrations.
- **Scalability for Mid-Sized Ops**: Handle 640-user concurrency peaks during quarter-ends, with Node.js microservices scaling horizontally; low-latency (<200ms) for London-based HQ and remote EU sites.
- **Integration Depth**: Native hooks for their stack—TypeScript SDKs, React components for embedding, Node.js middleware; ETL from GCP BigQuery to centralized lake for sales forecasting.
- **Budget-Aligned Customization**: Features capped at $75K annual value, prioritizing MVP with engineering/sales unification over advanced ML; easy self-serve config for ops team.
- **Reporting and Analytics**: Custom KPIs like part yield rates (eng) vs. quote win rates (sales), visualized in React apps; exportable to tools like Tableau for board-level reviews.
- **Reliability SLOs**: 99.9% uptime, with GCP failover; audit trails for security compliance, addressing concerns from their architecture that led to downtime incidents last quarter.

These requirements were refined iteratively, stemming from their pain points and stack constraints.

## Competitive Landscape

CastPoint is evaluating three alternatives alongside us:

- **SyncForge**: Incumbent for sales CRM integrations; strong on React/Node.js but weak on GCP/MongoDB security, per Emily's feedback. They're locked in but facing renewal pushback due to silos.
- **DataLink Pro**: GCP-native tool from Google Cloud Marketplace; excels in vendor lock-in avoidance ironically, but lacks cross-team unification for manufacturing workflows—Raj cited poor React embeddability.
- **NexusHub**: Open-source darling with TypeScript support; budget-friendly but unproven at 640-employee scale, with security concerns mirroring CastPoint's current issues. Tom Xu dismissed it for lacking enterprise SLAs.

Our edge lies in holistic pain point resolution—silos, security, lock-in—tailored to their London ops and stack. No single competitor checks all boxes, positioning us for win assuming RFP responsiveness.

## Risk Factors

- **Priority Shifts**: The board meeting last month introduced cost-cutting mandates, potentially squeezing the $75K budget if economic headwinds worsen; their 640-employee size amplifies sensitivity to revenue dips in manufacturing.
- **Technical Hurdles**: Ongoing security concerns with GCP architecture could delay POC if our integrations trigger false positives in audits—Raj has flagged this as a red line, and it might require extra engineering cycles.
- **Stakeholder Misalignment**: Data silos persist culturally; sales (Tom Xu) vs. eng (Emily) friction could resurface, especially since no single decision maker owns the process, risking veto at late stages. They discussed it and agreed it was critical, but follow-through is uncertain.
