# SkillPulse — Account Overview

## Company Background
SkillPulse is a dynamic EdTech company specializing in AI-powered learning platforms that personalize educational experiences for K-12 and higher education institutions worldwide. Founded in 2018 by a team of former Google engineers and educators frustrated with outdated learning management systems, the company has grown rapidly to approximately 136 employees, operating primarily out of San Francisco with a remote-first culture. With annual revenue hovering around $161 million, SkillPulse powers adaptive tutoring for over 5 million students across 2,000 school districts, leveraging cutting-edge AI to boost engagement and outcomes. Their recent 18% increase in IT budget this fiscal year underscores a commitment to scaling infrastructure amid aggressive expansion, though they've grappled with compliance audit failures on data handling that have delayed product rollouts. The company's tech stack, centered on Go for backend services, Kubernetes for orchestration, AWS for cloud hosting, DynamoDB for NoSQL storage, and gRPC for microservices communication, supports their high-velocity development needs but has exposed pain points in developer workflows.

## Technical Requirements
- **Developer Experience Enhancement**: Tools to mitigate poor developer experience by automating integration between Go services, gRPC APIs, and Salesforce Apex triggers, reducing onboarding time from weeks to days and accelerating feature velocity.
- **Compliance and Data Handling**: Built-in auditing and policy enforcement for data flows across AWS, Kubernetes, and DynamoDB to prevent future compliance audit failures, with automated reporting for regulatory standards like FERPA and GDPR relevant to EdTech.
- **Observability and Monitoring**: Real-time metrics dashboards for tracking developer productivity, service latencies in gRPC calls, and DynamoDB query performance, integrated with existing Kubernetes logging pipelines.
- **AI ROI Measurement**: Analytics modules to quantify impact of AI-driven features, such as engagement uplift in learning platforms, with customizable KPIs tied to revenue metrics amid their $161M ARR growth.
- **Scalability Support**: Horizontal scaling recommendations for their 136-employee engineering team, supporting high-traffic student sessions without Apex trigger bottlenecks.
- **Seamless Salesforce Integration**: No-code/low-code bridges to replace custom Apex triggers, ensuring bidirectional sync with SkillPulse's core Go backend.
- **Security Posture**: Role-based access controls and encryption at rest/transit for sensitive student data, addressing recent audit gaps.
- **POC Readiness**: Environment setup compatible with AWS VPCs, Kubernetes namespaces, and gRPC protobuf schemas for rapid evaluation by Ivan's team.
- **Budget Alignment**: Solutions scalable within their 18% increased IT spend, prioritizing quick wins on velocity and compliance.
