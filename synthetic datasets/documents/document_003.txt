# CareStack — Account Overview

## Company Background
CareStack is a Healthcare Tech company specializing in cloud-based dental practice management software, serving small to mid-sized dental practices across North America. With approximately 24 employees—falling within the 11-50 company size range—they focus on streamlining administrative tasks, patient scheduling, billing, and analytics for dental offices, helping practices reduce no-shows, optimize revenue cycles, and improve patient experiences through intuitive digital tools. Founded in 2018 by a team of dentists and software engineers who were frustrated with fragmented legacy systems in the dental industry, CareStack emerged from Orlando, Florida, initially as a bootstrapped startup targeting underserved solo practitioners. The company's growth accelerated during the pandemic as telehealth and remote management became critical, leading to their current tech stack built on C#, .NET for backend services, Azure for cloud hosting, SQL Server for data persistence, and RabbitMQ for message queuing to handle real-time workflows. Despite their innovative approach, they've grappled with internal data silos between engineering and sales teams, which has hindered cross-functional visibility into customer needs and product roadmaps. Recently, their IT budget was increased by 30% this fiscal year to support modernization efforts, including a planned migration off legacy systems by the end of the year.

## Technical Requirements
CareStack's needs center on resolving data silos, legacy integrations, and analytics gaps within their C#, .NET, Azure, SQL Server, and RabbitMQ environment. Key requirements include:

- **Seamless Data Synchronization**: Bi-directional syncing between engineering databases (SQL Server) and sales CRMs to eliminate silos, supporting real-time updates via RabbitMQ queues without custom middleware.
- **Legacy System Integration**: Low-code connectors for migrating off outdated on-prem systems by end-of-year, handling complex ETL processes from legacy .NET monoliths to Azure-hosted microservices.
- **Real-Time Analytics Dashboard**: Customizable dashboards pulling from Azure data lakes, providing sales teams with live metrics on onboarding pipelines—critical after losing a key customer last quarter to delays.
- **API-First Architecture Compatibility**: RESTful and GraphQL endpoints fully compatible with C#/.NET clients, including OAuth2 auth flows and webhook support for RabbitMQ event-driven triggers.
- **Scalability for Growth**: Horizontal scaling on Azure to support their 11-50 employee size, with auto-provisioning for up to 500 concurrent dental practice users.
- **Compliance and Security**: HIPAA-compliant data handling, with Azure Key Vault integration, audit logs, and role-based access mirroring their current SQL Server RBAC setups.
- **Onboarding Automation**: Workflow builders to automate patient and billing migrations, reducing manual interventions that plagued their recent customer churn.
- **Custom Reporting**: Ad-hoc query tools over federated data sources, bridging engineering telemetry and sales KPIs for unified insights.
- **Monitoring and Alerts**: Integration with Azure Monitor and RabbitMQ management plugins for proactive issue detection in high-volume messaging scenarios.
- **Migration Tooling**: Phased rollout scripts for legacy-to-cloud transitions, with rollback capabilities to minimize downtime during EOY push.

These align closely with their 30% IT budget uplift, prioritizing quick wins in analytics and silos.
