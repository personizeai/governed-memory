# MediaForge — Account Overview

## Company Background
MediaForge is a dynamic digital media company headquartered in San Francisco, specializing in advanced content processing, analytics, and distribution platforms for streaming services and ad tech firms. With approximately 189 employees—placing it firmly in the 51-200 employee size category—the company has carved out a niche in handling large-scale media workflows, leveraging cutting-edge data technologies to optimize video encoding, metadata enrichment, and real-time personalization. Founded in 2018 by a trio of ex-Google engineers who identified gaps in scalable media pipelines during their time building YouTube's backend infrastructure, MediaForge quickly scaled from a startup garage operation to a key player in the industry. The company's growth has been steady, though not without challenges; notably, they lost a major customer last quarter due to significant onboarding issues that disrupted service delivery and exposed limitations in their legacy systems. This setback has heightened their focus on robust, vendor-supported solutions. Bolstering their technical capabilities, MediaForge's engineering team is in expansion mode, having hired 10 new engineers this quarter to address surging demands in data-intensive media processing. Their tech stack, centered around Scala for high-performance applications, Spark for big data processing, Databricks for collaborative analytics, Snowflake for cloud data warehousing, and Airflow for workflow orchestration, underscores a mature yet evolving infrastructure poised for enterprise-grade upgrades.

## Technical Requirements
- **Scalable Data Ingestion**: Replace brittle internal Python scripts pulling from 15+ media and ad APIs with a unified, fault-tolerant pipeline supporting 10TB+ daily volumes, fully integrable with existing Scala/Spark services.
- **Workflow Orchestration**: Airflow-compatible scheduling with advanced retry logic and monitoring to prevent the onboarding failures seen last quarter.
- **Analytics Integration**: Native connectors to Databricks for ML-based content recommendation and Snowflake for querying petabyte-scale media metadata.
- **Real-Time Processing**: Low-latency Spark streaming enhancements for personalization features, handling spikes without downtime.
- **Security & Compliance**: SOC2 compliance, RBAC for multi-team access (growing eng team of recent 10 hires), and audit trails for regulated media content.
- **POC Scope**: 4-week pilot ingesting sample APIs, benchmarking against current scripts, with success metrics tied to 50% latency reduction.
- **Extensibility**: Future-proof for emerging formats like AV1 encoding, aligning with San Francisco HQ's innovation focus.
