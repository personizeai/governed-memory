# StreamOps — Account Overview

## Company Background
StreamOps is a prominent digital media company headquartered in their main office in London, employing around 2009 people globally, which places them firmly in the 1000+ employee category. Specializing in real-time content distribution platforms for video streaming and interactive media experiences, they power everything from live sports broadcasts to on-demand entertainment services for major broadcasters and content creators. Founded in 2015 by a trio of former BBC engineers who identified gaps in scalable media processing pipelines, StreamOps quickly scaled from a bootstrapped startup to a key player in the industry by leveraging agile development practices and cloud-native architectures; their growth trajectory has been fueled by strategic partnerships with European telcos and a focus on low-latency delivery. Today, their tech stack revolves around TypeScript for frontend robustness, React for dynamic UIs, Node.js for backend services, Google Cloud Platform (GCP) for hosting, and MongoDB for flexible data storage, enabling them to handle petabytes of media data annually. Maria Rivera, the Head of Data who reports directly to the CEO, has been instrumental in this evolution during her 4 years at the company, championing data-driven decisions amid their shift away from legacy systems.

## Technical Requirements
- **Real-time Stream Processing**: Must handle high-volume media streams with sub-second latency, integrating natively with GCP and Node.js backends to replace spreadsheets and manual data entry.
- **Data Analytics Dashboard**: Customizable UI built on React/TypeScript, supporting MongoDB queries for operational insights, with role-based access compliant for the upcoming Q2 security audit.
- **Scalability and Reliability**: Auto-scaling on GCP to support 2009-employee operations from London HQ, including failover mechanisms tested in POC phase.
- **Security and Compliance**: Features for audit logging and encryption, essential given Maria Rivera's direct reporting to the CEO on data governance after her 4 years stabilizing their stack.
- **API Integrations**: RESTful endpoints compatible with existing Node.js services, enabling two-way sync without disrupting current workflows.
- **Monitoring and Alerts**: Proactive notifications for pipeline anomalies, far beyond their manual processes, with historical data retention for forensic analysis.
- **Customization**: Low-code configuration for media-specific metrics, aligning with their digital media focus and evaluation of tools like Outreach for sales alignment.
- **Performance Benchmarks**: Must demonstrate 99.99% uptime in POC, outperforming Clari/ZoomInfo in real-time capabilities during the technical phase before business case presentation.
- **Deployment Simplicity**: Kubernetes-ready on GCP, minimizing DevOps overhead for Brandon Costa's infrastructure team.
- **Cost Efficiency**: Usage-based pricing to fit Derek Oduya's budget, with ROI projections tied to reduced manual entry errors.
