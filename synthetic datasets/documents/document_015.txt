# AdStack — Account Overview

## Company Background

AdStack is a prominent digital media company specializing in programmatic advertising solutions, enabling brands and agencies to optimize ad placements across premium digital channels. With approximately 493 employees, the organization falls squarely within the mid-market segment (201-1000 headcount), operating primarily in the competitive ad tech space where real-time bidding, audience targeting, and performance analytics drive revenue growth. Founded in 2015 by a trio of former Google engineers who identified gaps in scalable ad serving infrastructure, AdStack quickly scaled from a bootstrapped startup in San Francisco to a venture-backed leader serving over 500 enterprise clients globally. The company's core platform processes billions of ad impressions daily, leveraging a modern tech stack that includes Ruby on Rails for its backend services, Heroku for cloud deployment, PostgreSQL for robust data persistence, and Sidekiq for efficient background job processing. Over the years, AdStack has navigated challenges like evolving privacy regulations and market consolidation, but it continues to thrive by focusing on data-driven personalization. Recently, they've been grappling with internal pain points such as compliance audit failures on data handling, which have exposed vulnerabilities in their data pipelines, and customer churn stemming from slow support response times that hinder client retention efforts.

## Technical Requirements

AdStack's technical needs are shaped by their mid-sized digital media operations and specific pain points. Key requirements include:

- **Real-time Data Integration**: Must support ingestion from Ruby on Rails apps on Heroku, with direct connectors to PostgreSQL for querying sales and support data, eliminating manual spreadsheet exports that currently create silos between engineering and sales.
- **Background Job Processing**: Compatibility with Sidekiq for handling high-volume tasks like churn prediction alerts and support ticket routing, reducing response times from days to minutes.
- **Compliance and Audit Logging**: Automated audit trails for all data handling to address recent compliance audit failures, including GDPR/CCPA-compliant retention policies and immutable logs viewable via a dashboard.
- **Scalability for Ad Tech Workloads**: Handle billions of daily events without inflating infrastructure costs, with auto-scaling on Heroku-like environments to protect margins currently eroded by high bills.
- **Churn Analytics Dashboard**: AI-driven insights into customer churn patterns linked to support delays, integrating with existing CRM workflows for proactive interventions.
- **Custom Reporting**: Replace spreadsheets with no-code builders for analytics teams, supporting exports to tools like Clari or Intercom if needed during transition.
- **Security Posture**: SOC 2 Type II compliance, with SSO and RBAC to pass their standard procurement security review.
- **API-First Architecture**: RESTful APIs for embedding into their Rails monolith, ensuring minimal disruption during POC.
- **Performance Monitoring**: Built-in metrics for query latency on PostgreSQL datasets exceeding 1TB in size.
- **Mobile Accessibility**: Dashboards optimized for field sales engineers tracking churn metrics on-the-go.

These align with their Qualification-stage evaluation, focusing on proof-of-concept viability before legal and VP Finance sign-off.
