# DataPulse Systems — Account Overview

## Company Background
DataPulse Systems is a B2B SaaS company specializing in real-time data analytics platforms for mid-market enterprises, particularly in the fintech and e-commerce sectors. With approximately 174 employees, fitting squarely into the 51-200 company size bracket, they provide scalable solutions that help businesses process and visualize streaming data for operational insights. Founded in 2015 by a team of former Oracle engineers frustrated with legacy database limitations, the company bootstrapped its way to profitability by focusing on Kafka-integrated tools that addressed gaps in event-driven architectures. Today, DataPulse powers feature velocity for dozens of clients, but internally, they're grappling with outdated processes like spreadsheets and manual data entry that hinder their own growth. Their journey from a Seattle garage startup to a key player in data orchestration underscores a commitment to innovation, though recent scaling challenges have exposed vulnerabilities in developer workflows.

## Technical Requirements
- **Real-Time Analytics Integration**: Need dashboards pulling live data from Kafka streams to replace spreadsheets and manual data entry, enabling sub-minute latency for decision-making across fintech clients.
- **Tech Stack Compatibility**: Full support for Java/Spring Boot applications deployed on Azure, with native connectors to Oracle databases; must handle high-throughput event processing without custom coding.
- **Developer Experience Enhancements**: Tools to monitor and optimize workflows, addressing poor developer experience that's currently slowing feature velocity by 30-40% per sprint, including IDE plugins and automated metrics.
- **Scalability for 174-Employee Org**: Handle 50+ devs generating 1M+ events daily; low-latency queries on historical Oracle data blended with real-time Kafka feeds.
- **Security and Compliance**: Azure AD integration, SOC 2 compliance, and role-based access for CPO-level oversight like Lucia Moreau's.
- **Budget-Aligned Deployment**: On-prem hybrid option initially, scaling to full SaaS; total allocated approximately $120k annually.
- **Ease of Migration**: Phased rollout from current manual processes, with training for infrastructure team led by Sarah Nakamura.

DataPulse Systems is actively evaluating several revenue intelligence and analytics platforms:

- **Chorus.ai (ZoomInfo)**: Considered for conversation intelligence; budget fits, but dev experience tools are underdeveloped compared to our offerings.
Our differentiation lies in seamless Java/Spring Boot and Azure support, directly tackling their manual data entry woes—something competitors gloss over.
