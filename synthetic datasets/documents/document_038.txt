# CargoNova — Account Overview

## Company Background

CargoNova is a mid-sized logistics company specializing in multimodal freight forwarding and supply chain optimization, serving clients across North America and Europe. With approximately 588 employees, the firm operates a network of warehouses and distribution centers, leveraging advanced routing algorithms to manage everything from perishable goods to heavy industrial cargo. Founded in 2012 by former executives from a major shipping conglomerate who identified gaps in real-time visibility for cross-border shipments, CargoNova has grown steadily through strategic acquisitions and a focus on technology-driven efficiencies. Their tech stack, which includes Rust for high-performance backend services, Actix for web frameworks, AWS for cloud infrastructure, PostgreSQL for data persistence, and NATS for messaging, underscores their commitment to scalable, resilient systems amid a competitive logistics landscape. Recently, they've faced challenges, including losing a major customer last quarter due to onboarding delays that highlighted weaknesses in their current tools.

## Technical Requirements

CargoNova's requirements are driven by their logistics workload, demanding robust, scalable solutions that integrate seamlessly with their established tech ecosystem. Key needs include:

- **Real-time Data Synchronization**: Full compatibility with Rust and Actix for backend processing, ensuring sub-second latency for shipment tracking via NATS pub-sub messaging.
- **Cloud-Native Scalability**: Deep AWS integration (e.g., Lambda, RDS for PostgreSQL) to handle peak volumes during holiday shipping seasons, replacing the limitations of Notion and Airtable.
- **Advanced Analytics Dashboards**: Customizable BI tools for supply chain visibility, with role-based access controls to mitigate risks exposed by last quarter's customer churn.
- **Onboarding Automation**: Workflow builders to streamline vendor and client integrations, directly addressing the onboarding issues that led to their major account loss.
- **Security and Compliance Features**: Audit-ready logging and encryption standards, critical for their Q2 security audit, including SOC 2 alignment and PostgreSQL query optimization for sensitive logistics data.
- **API-First Design**: RESTful and GraphQL endpoints compatible with Actix-web, plus event-driven hooks into NATS for microservices orchestration.
- **Multi-Tenant Support**: Isolation for their 588-employee user base across departments, with admin controls for Simone Mitchell's operations team.
- **Reporting and Export**: Native exports to PostgreSQL schemas and AWS S3 buckets, surpassing the ad-hoc exports from their current Notion-Airtable combo.
- **Mobile Accessibility**: Responsive apps for warehouse staff, integrated with AWS Cognito for auth.
- **Custom Integrations**: Plugins for logistics-specific ERPs, with extensibility to avoid the pitfalls of in-house builds they've considered.

These specs reflect their 27% IT budget uplift this fiscal year, positioning them for growth post-recent setbacks.
