# TechFlow Inc — Account Overview

## Company Background
TechFlow Inc is a B2B SaaS company specializing in workflow automation and data orchestration platforms for mid-market enterprises in the fintech and logistics sectors. Founded in 2015 by a team of ex-Google engineers frustrated with fragmented tools for scaling operations, the company has grown to approximately 166 employees, fitting squarely within the 51-200 size bracket that allows for agile decision-making without the bureaucracy of larger enterprises. Their platform enables seamless data flows across disparate systems, helping clients automate complex processes from onboarding to compliance reporting. Over the past decade, they've bootstrapped to profitability before raising a modest Series B, focusing on organic growth through enterprise wins in high-regulation industries. TechFlow's tech stack is modern and robust, leveraging Go for backend services, Kubernetes for orchestration, AWS for cloud infrastructure, DynamoDB for NoSQL storage, and gRPC for efficient microservices communication. This setup positions them well for the scalability demands of their customers, though they're currently grappling with integration complexity with their own legacy systems inherited from early acquisitions.

## Technical Requirements
- **Core Integration**: Must support frictionless connectivity with existing stack (Go, Kubernetes, AWS, DynamoDB, gRPC) while accommodating integration complexity from legacy systems during their end-of-year migration.
- **Automation for Onboarding**: Eliminate manual processes for customer onboarding, including automated workflows that reduce time from weeks to hours and mitigate compliance audit failures on data handling.
- **Scalability and Hiring Support**: Tools to augment engineering capacity amid difficulty hiring specialized engineers, with AI-assisted code generation for gRPC services and Kubernetes deployments.
- **Compliance and Security**: Built-in auditing for data pipelines in DynamoDB, ensuring no repeat of recent audit failures; must pass their rigorous security review in the standard procurement flow.
- **Performance Metrics**: Handle B2B SaaS workloads for 166-employee operation, with low-latency gRPC calls and AWS auto-scaling to support growth without additional headcount.
- **Migration Compatibility**: Phased rollout enabling migration off legacy systems by December 2026, with minimal downtime for their fintech/logistics clients.
- **Analytics Layer**: Real-time dashboards for monitoring hiring pipelines and onboarding efficiency, integrating with existing AWS services.

These requirements stem directly from pain points voiced across meetings, positioning our platform as a strategic enabler for their medium-urgency initiatives.
