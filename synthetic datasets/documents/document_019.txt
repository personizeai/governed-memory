# BioSignal AI — Account Overview

## Company Background

BioSignal AI is a cutting-edge healthcare technology company specializing in AI-driven analysis of biosignals, such as ECG, EEG, and EMG data, to enable real-time diagnostics and predictive analytics for hospitals and clinics worldwide. Founded in 2018 by a team of biomedical engineers and clinicians frustrated with the limitations of legacy medical monitoring systems, the company started in a small incubator in Boston and has since grown to approximately 45 employees, fitting squarely within the 11-50 employee range that positions it as a nimble mid-stage startup. Their mission is to democratize advanced signal processing through cloud-native AI platforms, helping healthcare providers reduce diagnostic errors by up to 30% according to their internal benchmarks. Over the past seven years, BioSignal AI has secured partnerships with three major hospital networks and expanded its engineering team significantly—recently hiring 13 new engineers this quarter alone to support scaling operations. This growth reflects their aggressive push into enterprise markets, where they process petabytes of sensitive patient data daily while navigating stringent HIPAA and GDPR compliance landscapes. The company's tech stack is modern and serverless-focused, leveraging Python for core ML models, FastAPI for APIs, AWS Lambda for event-driven compute, DynamoDB for NoSQL storage, and SQS for message queuing, which underscores their commitment to scalable, cost-efficient architectures. Despite this sophistication, they've encountered challenges with data handling that have led to compliance audit failures, prompting a reevaluation of their pipeline visibility and security posture. As they prepare for a critical security audit in the upcoming Q3, BioSignal AI is at an inflection point, balancing rapid innovation with enterprise-grade reliability.

## Technical Requirements

BioSignal AI's requirements are tailored to their healthcare tech environment, focusing on alleviating pain points while integrating seamlessly with their stack. Key needs include:

- **Real-time Pipeline Visibility**: Dashboards for monitoring Python-FastAPI endpoints, AWS Lambda invocations, DynamoDB query latencies, and SQS queue depths, addressing poor visibility into pipeline health that currently leaves their growing engineering team (recently bolstered by 13 hires) reactive rather than proactive.
- **Compliance and Audit Logging**: Automated, immutable logs for all data handling flows to prevent recurrence of compliance audit failures, with exportable reports in HIPAA-compliant formats for their upcoming Q3 security audit.
- **Security Enhancements**: Zero-trust access controls and encryption at-rest/in-transit for biosignal data, mitigating security concerns with current architecture and reducing vendor lock-in risks tied to AWS dependencies.
- **CRM Integration**: Native connectors to HubSpot CRM via Zapier automations, ensuring pipeline health metrics feed into sales forecasts without custom scripting.
- **Scalability for Growth**: Serverless-compatible monitoring that scales with their 45-employee operation's expansion, supporting high-throughput biosignal processing (up to 10,000 signals/minute).
- **POC Environment Setup**: Isolated AWS-like sandbox for Marcus's infrastructure team to test integrations, with metrics on Lambda cold starts and SQS backlogs, targeted for deployment next month.
- **Custom Reporting**: Alerts on anomalies in DynamoDB throughput or FastAPI response times, customizable for their product team's roadmap priorities.
- **Budget Alignment**: Solution must fit within ~$600,000 annual commitment, with ROI projections showing 40% reduction in audit remediation costs.

These requirements were refined post-deep dive, emphasizing urgency around the May 13 decision date.
