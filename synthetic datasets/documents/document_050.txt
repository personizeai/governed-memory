# VaultEdge — Account Overview

## Company Background
VaultEdge is a fintech startup specializing in secure digital vault solutions for small to mid-sized financial institutions and lending platforms. With approximately 28 employees, the company focuses on providing encrypted storage and real-time analytics for sensitive financial data, enabling clients to streamline compliance and risk management processes. Founded in 2018 by two former bankers, ex-employees of a major Wall Street firm who grew frustrated with outdated legacy systems in the industry, VaultEdge quickly gained traction through its innovative approach to data security. The company's rapid early growth led to significant technical debt, as they scaled from a bootstrapped operation to serving over 50 clients within three years. Today, they operate in a competitive fintech landscape, battling data silos between their engineering and sales teams that hinder pipeline visibility and collaborative forecasting. This internal friction, compounded by challenges in hiring specialized engineers, has prompted them to seek modern tools for integration and observability.

## Technical Requirements
- **Data Silo Resolution**: Need bidirectional integration to unify engineering telemetry (e.g., from Sidekiq job queues) with sales CRM data, eliminating silos that currently obscure pipeline health visibility.
- **Tech Stack Compatibility**: Full support for Ruby on Rails applications hosted on Heroku, with native connectors to PostgreSQL databases and Sidekiq for real-time monitoring without refactoring.
- **Migration Assistance**: Tools to facilitate a smooth transition off legacy systems by year-end, including automated schema mapping and zero-downtime deployment strategies tailored to their 28-employee fintech environment.
- **Pipeline Visibility Enhancements**: Dashboards providing engineering-sales cross-team insights, addressing poor visibility into deal progression and forecasting accuracy.
- **Hiring Friction Mitigation**: Features enabling non-specialist engineers to onboard quickly, countering difficulties in recruiting amid technical debt accumulated from rapid early growth.
- **Scalability for Growth**: Handle current load of ~10k daily transactions while scaling to support fintech compliance reporting, with alerts on debt hotspots in RoR/Heroku stacks.
- **Budget-Aligned Implementation**: Phased rollout within $50,000 envelope, prioritizing high-impact silo fixes first.
