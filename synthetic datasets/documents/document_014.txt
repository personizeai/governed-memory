# FreightPulse — Account Overview

## Company Background

FreightPulse is a dynamic logistics company specializing in digital freight forwarding and supply chain optimization, operating primarily in the North American market with about 60 employees. Founded in 2018 by a trio of former executives from established shipping giants—led by CEO Elena Vasquez, who identified gaps in real-time visibility for small-to-medium shippers—the company quickly disrupted the industry by launching a SaaS platform that connects shippers directly with carriers, streamlining quoting, booking, and tracking processes. What started as a bootstrapped MVP in a garage in Seattle has grown into a venture-backed operation with Series A funding secured in 2021, enabling rapid expansion amid the e-commerce boom during the pandemic. Today, FreightPulse handles over 10,000 shipments monthly, focusing on less-than-truckload (LTL) and parcel services for e-commerce retailers and manufacturers. However, this explosive early growth—doubling headcount in the first two years—has left them grappling with significant technical debt, including outdated monolithic applications that hinder scalability. Their engineering team is growing rapidly, having hired 6 people this quarter alone to tackle these challenges, as they plan a full migration off legacy systems by end of year. Currently, their tech stack revolves around Ruby on Rails for the core application, hosted on Heroku, with PostgreSQL as the primary database and Sidekiq for background job processing. This setup supports their agile operations but struggles with advanced analytics needs, particularly around measuring ROI on recent AI initiatives like predictive ETAs.

## Technical Requirements

* **Integration with Existing Stack**: Must support Ruby on Rails APIs natively, with SDKs for Heroku deployments, direct PostgreSQL querying via secure connectors, and Sidekiq compatibility for job queuing to avoid rework in their async workflows.
* **ROI Measurement for AI Initiatives**: Provide customizable dashboards tracking metrics like cost savings from AI routing, adoption rates, and predictive accuracy, addressing their core pain of unclear returns on tools like ETA forecasters—beyond what Microsoft Dynamics offers.
* **Legacy Migration Support**: Tools and consulting for phased migration off legacy systems by end of year, including data export from on-prem to cloud, with minimal downtime for their 10,000+ monthly shipments.
* **Scalability for Growing Team**: Handle increased load from engineering expansion (recently added 6 hires this quarter), supporting 51-200 employee scale with auto-scaling and low-latency queries on PostgreSQL datasets exceeding 1TB.
* **CRM Workflow Enhancement**: Augment Microsoft Dynamics and Power Automate with no-code automations, event triggers for Sidekiq, and Rails middleware plugins to reduce technical debt from rapid growth.
* **Real-Time Logistics Analytics**: Shipment tracking, carrier performance scoring, and anomaly detection integrated into their Heroku app, with export to PostgreSQL for Lisa Moreau's data team.
* **Security and Compliance**: SOC 2 compliance, role-based access for key contacts like Daniel Nakamura, and audit logs for VP-level oversight.
* **Custom Reporting**: ROI calculators tailored to logistics KPIs, pulling from Sidekiq-processed events and Rails models.
* **POC Feasibility**: Quick-start POC deployable on Heroku in under a week, validating against their full stack.
* **Vendor Support**: Dedicated onboarding for 6-year veteran Daniel and his team, with SLAs matching their high-volume ops.
