# CastPoint — Account Overview

## Company Background

CastPoint is a mid-sized manufacturing company specializing in precision-engineered components for the automotive and aerospace sectors, with a workforce of approximately 151 employees and annual revenue hovering around $41 million. Founded in 2015 by a team of engineers who identified gaps in the supply chain for high-tolerance metal parts, the company quickly scaled from a garage operation in Detroit to a key player in the Midwest manufacturing hub. Their core offerings include custom CNC machining, additive manufacturing services, and just-in-time inventory solutions tailored for OEMs facing volatile demand cycles. Over the years, CastPoint has invested heavily in data-driven operations to optimize production lines, leveraging their tech stack of Scala for backend services, Spark for big data processing, Databricks for collaborative analytics, Snowflake for cloud data warehousing, and Airflow for orchestrating complex ETL pipelines. This technological foundation supported their rapid early growth, but it has also introduced challenges like technical debt from hasty implementations during boom periods. Recently, they experienced customer churn, including the loss of a major client last quarter due to protracted onboarding issues that exposed weaknesses in their support response times. Despite these hurdles, CastPoint remains committed to innovation, positioning itself as a resilient partner in an industry plagued by supply disruptions and rising material costs. With a company size in the 51-200 employee range, they maintain a lean structure that emphasizes cross-functional teams, though scaling pains have become evident as they push toward $50M in revenue.

## Technical Requirements

- **Accelerated Deployment Pipelines**: Reduce current 2-3 week cycles to under 48 hours, integrating with Airflow and Databricks for automated CI/CD in Scala/Spark environments.
- **Enhanced Security Architecture**: Address vulnerabilities in existing setup via zero-trust models, encryption at rest/transit for Snowflake data, and compliance with manufacturing regs like ITAR.
- **Technical Debt Refactoring**: Tools to modernize legacy Scala codebases accumulated from rapid early growth, enabling modular Spark jobs without full rewrites.
- **Improved Support and Onboarding**: Sub-4-hour response SLAs to prevent customer churn, with self-service portals for faster manufacturing client integrations.
- **Scalable Analytics**: Seamless interoperability across Spark, Databricks, and Snowflake for real-time production insights, supporting 151-employee ops at $41M scale.
- **Budget-Conscious Implementation**: Solutions fitting within ~$120K envelope, prioritizing POC validation before leadership buy-in.
