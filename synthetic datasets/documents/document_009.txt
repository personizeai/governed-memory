# SyncWave — Account Overview

## Company Background
SyncWave is a B2B SaaS company specializing in real-time data synchronization solutions for distributed sales teams, helping enterprises streamline pipeline forecasting and revenue operations across global operations. Founded in 2019 by a group of former sales executives from Chicago's fintech scene who identified gaps in legacy CRM tools during their time at larger firms, the company has grown to approximately 37 employees, fitting squarely within the 11-50 employee size bracket that positions it as an agile mid-stage startup. Headquartered in Chicago, SyncWave focuses on delivering lightweight, scalable sync engines that integrate seamlessly with modern cloud infrastructures, enabling sales organizations to achieve hyper-accurate visibility into deal progression without the overhead of heavy custom builds. Their rapid ascent is fueled by a 21% increase in IT budget this fiscal year, allowing them to invest aggressively in tools that address core pain points like poor visibility into pipeline health. Today, they serve a niche of mid-market B2B clients in the software and services sectors, emphasizing low-latency data flows that have become mission-critical in volatile economic climates.

The conversation wrapped with enthusiasm for a POC, and they noted their IT budget had seen a healthy 21% uplift this year, signaling strong internal buy-in. It was clear from their tone that urgency was critical, as pipeline blind spots were directly impacting quarterly forecasts.

## Technical Requirements
- **Real-time Pipeline Visibility**: Must provide granular, AI-driven insights into deal stages and health scores, surpassing limitations of current HubSpot CRM with Zapier automations that result in delayed or incomplete data syncing.
- **Seamless Legacy Integration**: Support for bridging complex integrations with on-prem legacy systems without requiring full rip-and-replace, addressing their primary pain point of integration complexity.
- **Cloud-Native Compatibility**: Full alignment with Java/Micronaut frameworks on GCP, including native support for Spanner databases and Pub/Sub messaging to minimize custom dev work.
- **Cost Optimization**: Reduce high infrastructure costs eating into margins via lightweight, scalable sync agents that avoid the bloat of heavier platforms.
- **Custom Forecasting Models**: Ability to ingest and synchronize sales data for predictive pipeline health scoring, tailored to SyncWave's 37-person team's forecasting cadences.
- **POC Environment Setup**: Sandbox for Heather Reyes' sales team to evaluate sync performance with live Chicago-based data feeds within two weeks.
- **Security and Compliance**: SOC 2 Type II compliance with fine-grained access controls for multi-tenant GCP environments.
- **Scalability for Growth**: Handle projected 2x pipeline volume increase without proportional cost spikes, leveraging their boosted IT budget.
- **Reporting Dashboards**: Intuitive, embeddable dashboards for CRO-level visibility into revenue risks.
- **Automation Workflows**: Replace Zapier hacks with native, no-code automations for trigger-based syncing across sales and ops tools.

## Competitive Landscape
SyncWave is actively evaluating a mix of established players and in-house options:
- **Clari**: Strong contender for revenue intelligence, but they've flagged it as too expensive for their margins and lacking Micronaut/GCP depth.
- **Salesforce Einstein**: Integrated with HubSpot via Zapier currently, but customization for legacy systems proves cumbersome and cost-prohibitive.
- **Custom Internal Tool**: A Java-based prototype they're iterating on, which offers flexibility but suffers scalability issues with Spanner/Pub/Sub at volume—our POC aims to de-risk switching.

This field is narrowing, with our proposal emphasizing lower TCO and faster time-to-value.
