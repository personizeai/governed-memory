# StreamOps — Account Overview

## Company Background
StreamOps is a prominent digital media company headquartered in its main office in Chicago, employing about 2811 people across its operations. Specializing in content streaming, live event broadcasting, and digital advertising platforms, the company delivers scalable media solutions to broadcasters, entertainment networks, and online publishers worldwide. Founded in 2015 by a group of former executives from a major cable network who identified gaps in real-time data processing for live streams, StreamOps quickly scaled from a startup garage operation to a mid-sized enterprise by leveraging innovative cloud-native architectures. Today, with a workforce exceeding 1000 employees—specifically around 2811—their engineering team is in a growth phase, having hired 12 new developers just this quarter to support expansion. Their technology stack heavily features Java and Spring Boot for backend services, Azure for cloud infrastructure, Oracle databases for enterprise data management, and Kafka for high-throughput event streaming, which aligns well with our platform's capabilities. As they plan a full migration off legacy on-premises systems by the end of 2026, StreamOps is positioning itself to handle increasing demands from 5G-enabled live events and personalized content delivery.

## Technical Requirements
StreamOps has outlined clear needs tied to their digital media workflows, migration goals, and tech stack. Key requirements include:
- **Real-time Event Streaming**: Must ingest and process high-volume Kafka streams from live events with sub-100ms latency, integrating natively with Java/Spring Boot services deployed on Azure.
- **Database Compatibility**: Seamless querying and syncing with Oracle databases for user analytics and content metadata, supporting hybrid cloud/on-prem setups during their end-of-year legacy migration.
- **Scalability for Growth**: Horizontal scaling to handle traffic spikes (e.g., 10x during major broadcasts), accommodating their engineering team's recent expansion of 12 hires this quarter.
- **Security and Compliance**: SOC 2 Type II, GDPR compliance, role-based access controls (RBAC), and audit logs for media IP protection; must pass Maya's rigorous technical evaluation.
- **UI/UX for Ops Teams**: Intuitive dashboards for non-technical end users like Yusuf's operations group in Chicago, with alerting on stream anomalies and one-click troubleshooting.
- **Migration Support**: Tools for zero-downtime cutover from legacy systems, including data replay from Kafka topics and schema evolution for Oracle schemas.
- **API-First Integration**: RESTful and gRPC endpoints compatible with their microservices architecture, plus webhooks for CRM workflows.
- **Monitoring and Observability**: Built-in metrics export to Azure Monitor, with SLO tracking for 99.99% uptime on streaming pipelines.
- **Cost Predictability**: Usage-based pricing capped at their ~$200,000 annual budget, with reserved capacity options for predictable media seasons.
- **Custom Extensibility**: Plugins or SDKs allowing extensions to their internal custom tools without vendor lock-in.

These align closely with our core strengths, positioning us favorably against alternatives.

Our edge lies in full-stack alignment with their tech (Java, Spring Boot, Azure, Oracle, Kafka) and migration tooling, which competitors can't match without heavy customization. They've deprioritized the custom tool internally.
