# SyncWave — Account Overview

## Company Background
SyncWave is a fast-growing B2B SaaS company operating in the sales enablement and revenue operations space, with a company size of 51-200 employees, specifically around 120 team members across engineering, sales, and customer success functions. Founded in 2018 by a trio of former sales executives from Salesforce and HubSpot who identified gaps in real-time pipeline forecasting tools, SyncWave has positioned itself as a provider of AI-driven analytics platforms that help mid-market sales teams optimize deal cycles and forecasting accuracy. Headquartered in Austin, Texas, the company bootstrapped initially through consulting gigs before raising a $15M Series A in 2021, which fueled expansion into Europe and Asia. Their core product, WaveForecast, integrates with CRM systems to deliver predictive insights, but recent challenges like poor visibility into pipeline health and compliance audit failures on data handling have prompted them to seek advanced solutions like ours. SyncWave's tech stack, built primarily on TypeScript for frontend reliability, React for dynamic UIs, Node.js for scalable backends, Google Cloud Platform (GCP) for hosting, and MongoDB for flexible data storage, underscores their modern, cloud-native approach to development.

## Technical Requirements
- **Pipeline Visibility**: Real-time dashboards and AI-driven alerts for deal health, addressing poor visibility into pipeline metrics; must integrate with existing CRM via Node.js APIs to flag risks like slippage or stalled opportunities.
- **Compliance and Data Handling**: Automated auditing tools to prevent failures like their recent SOC 2 issues; support for secure data pipelines in GCP environments, with MongoDB schema validation and encryption at rest/transit.
- **Deployment Acceleration**: CI/CD optimizations to reduce slow deployment cycles from 2-3 weeks per release; compatibility with TypeScript, React, and Node.js stacks for seamless frontend/backend deployments.
- **Scalability for 51-200 Employee Org**: Handle 120+ users across sales and ops, with low-latency querying on large datasets stored in MongoDB.
- **Integration Depth**: Native support for GCP services, including Cloud Functions and Pub/Sub for event-driven pipeline updates.
- **Customization**: React-compatible UI components for embedding WaveForecast-like insights into their internal tools.
- **Security Posture**: Zero-trust architecture with RBAC, aligning with their post-audit compliance mandates.

## Competitive Landscape
SyncWave has been actively evaluating a shortlist of competitors to address their pipeline visibility and compliance gaps:
- **Microsoft Copilot**: Strong contender for its Office 365 integrations and lightweight AI forecasting; Sarah noted its appeal for quick wins but concerns over customization limits in their GCP-centric environment.

We've differentiated on full-stack compatibility (e.g., TypeScript and React support) and faster deployments, positioning us ahead in their internal scorecard.
