# MedCore Analytics — Account Overview

## Company Background

MedCore Analytics is a healthcare technology company specializing in advanced data analytics platforms for medical providers and insurers. With approximately 48 employees, the firm operates in the competitive HealthTech sector, focusing on tools that aggregate patient data, predict outcomes, and optimize resource allocation across hospitals and clinics. Founded in 2018 by a team of former hospital administrators and data scientists frustrated with fragmented legacy systems in healthcare, MedCore bootstrapped its initial growth through grants and early contracts with regional providers. The company's annual revenue hovers around $68 million, driven by subscription-based analytics services that help clients reduce operational costs by up to 25%. Over the past few years, they've expanded from a small Seattle-based startup to a mid-sized player, but recent challenges, including the loss of a major customer last quarter due to onboarding issues, have prompted a strategic review. They're now planning a full migration off their legacy systems by the end of the year, aiming to modernize their infrastructure amid shifting board priorities.

## Technical Requirements

- **Data Aggregation and ETL**: Must ingest and normalize data from 10+ healthcare APIs (e.g., FHIR-compliant sources, claims processors) currently handled by fragile internal Python scripts; support for scheduled pulls via Sidekiq integration required to avoid queue overflows.
- **Real-Time Analytics Dashboards**: Customizable dashboards for sales and ops teams, with sub-second latency on PostgreSQL queries; must scale to 48 users without Heroku dyno spikes.
- **Migration Compatibility**: Seamless integration with existing Ruby on Rails apps on Heroku; tools for exporting legacy data during end-of-year migration, minimizing downtime to under 4 hours.
- **Security and Compliance**: HIPAA-compliant encryption at rest/transit; audit logs for all API interactions, addressing recent onboarding failures that lost a key customer.
- **Scalability and Monitoring**: Auto-scaling job queues replacing Sidekiq bottlenecks; alerting for API failures, tied to their ~$68M revenue ops workflows.
- **ROI Reporting**: Built-in metrics dashboard showing cost savings (target: 20% reduction in script maintenance); budget-gated at approximately $850,000 annually.
- **Custom Integrations**: Plugins for PostgreSQL extensions and Rails middleware; support for board-mandated priority shifts like predictive modeling add-ons.

These requirements stem directly from their stack and recent pains, with emphasis on replacing Python scripts that pull from multiple sources inefficiently.
