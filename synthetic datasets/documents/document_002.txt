# LedgerPoint — Account Overview

## Company Background
LedgerPoint is a fintech startup specializing in real-time ledger management and reconciliation services for mid-sized financial institutions. With approximately 34 employees, the company operates in the competitive SaaS space for banking automation, helping clients streamline transaction processing and compliance reporting. Founded in 2020 by former bankers frustrated with outdated reconciliation tools during the pandemic, LedgerPoint bootstrapped its initial product—a cloud-based ledger aggregator—before raising a modest Series A round in 2022 to fuel product development. Today, they serve over 50 financial clients across North America and Europe, but rapid growth has strained their internal infrastructure, particularly as their engineering team is growing and they hired 7 people this quarter to handle increased demand for custom features. The company's size places it firmly in the 11-50 employee range, positioning it as an agile player in fintech but one grappling with scaling pains like high infrastructure costs eating into margins.

## Technical Requirements
- **API Consolidation and Automation**: Replace internal Python scripts pulling from multiple APIs with a unified platform to automate data reconciliation and reduce manual intervention in customer onboarding processes.
- **Scalability for Growth**: Support for expanding engineering teams, including seamless handling of increased transaction volumes as they integrate new hires—recently 7 this quarter—without proportional infrastructure cost spikes.
- **Migration from Legacy Systems**: Tools for smooth migration off legacy reconciliation systems by end of 2026, minimizing downtime and addressing vendor lock-in with their current cloud provider.
- **Cost Optimization**: Features to combat high infrastructure costs eating into margins, such as dynamic resource scaling and usage-based pricing models.
- **Compliance and Security**: Robust audit trails and encryption to prepare for their upcoming Q3 security audit, ensuring fintech-grade compliance for real-time ledger management.
- **Onboarding Efficiency**: Streamlined workflows to eliminate manual processes for customer onboarding, integrating directly with their core banking APIs.
- **Custom Reporting**: Real-time dashboards for product and operations teams, customizable for their 34-employee fintech environment.
